## Semantic audit
The refuted clause (C) is "for every sofic shift X, the number of states of its Fischer (minimal right-resolving) presentation equals the rank of the Hankel matrix of L(X)". The counterexample is the even shift, the canonical introductory example of symbolic dynamics: its Fischer cover is the familiar 2-vertex graph (A —0→ A, A —1→ B, B —1→ A), which the submission proves correct in both directions (a state-alternation argument for soundness, an explicit parity-based state assignment for completeness) together with the fact that no 0- or 1-vertex labelled graph presents X (the three one-loop cases are each excluded by an explicit point). On the other side, the Hankel matrix of the even shift contains the 3×3 minor on rows {11, 0, 01} and columns {0, 10, 1} equal to [[1,1,1],[1,0,1],[0,1,1]] with determinant −1 and an integral inverse, so the rank is ≥ 3 over every field; the row analysis (follower sets F(u) ∈ {∅, L(X), F(0), F(01)} according to the parity of the trailing run of 1s after the last 0) gives rank ≤ 3. Hence rank = 3 ≠ 2 = Fis(X) over every field. This is mathematically airtight — the key point being that the row space over a field of a 0/1 matrix of unions of follower sets need not be spanned by the vertex indicators (that is only true over the Boolean semiring), and my independent computations confirm every quantitative claim, including the mod-p ranks.

The Lean formalization is faithful: `Claim` quantifies over all sofic X and all m with `IsFisherCount X m` (least number of vertices of a right-resolving presentation), asserting `HankelRankIs X m` (rank exactly m: at least m and not at least m+1). The refutation instantiates the even shift (proved sofic), its Fisher count 2, and `hankel_rank_ge_three` (a 3×3 minor with nonzero determinant, which contradicts "not at least 3"); variants refute the ℤ/p reading for every p ≥ 2, the arbitrary-presentation reading, and give a second, stronger counterexample (the charge-constrained shift X_c with Fischer count ≤ 3 and Hankel rank ≥ 5 via a determinant-1 minor, which also separates the follower-set-graph reading: 6 nonempty follower sets ≠ 5). The rank notion (`HankelRankAtLeast` = some r×r minor with nonzero determinant) is the standard field-rank characterization; determinant −1/±1 makes the counterexample field-independent.

The reading analysis is exemplary and honest: field rank is correctly identified as the standard meaning of "Hankel rank" (Kronecker, Fliess, Carlyle–Paz, Ito); the Boolean-semiring reading is explicitly NOT claimed refuted (the Boolean rank of the even shift's H is 2 = its Fischer count, and Boolean rank ≤ Fischer count always, so the clause may be true in that reading — a disclosed limitation, not a defect, since Boolean rank is a different, always-so-named notion); HMM/probability readings are analyzed and shown measure-dependent. Since the conjecture's clause as written uses the standard rank, the disproof stands.
