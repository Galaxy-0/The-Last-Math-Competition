CONVENTIONS AND EXACT NECESSARY CLAUSE

The result takes S to be an arbitrary subset of the usual real line, with its
standard Euclidean metric, and uses algebraic independence and transcendence
over Q. The source does not literally print R or Q. The supported ordinary
number-theoretic convention is declared explicitly in the Lean source and here;
no arbitrary-field or complex-ambient coverage is claimed.

The first clause says that every algebraically independent set of transcendental
numbers has Hausdorff dimension 1. Its formal statement is exactly:

  forall S : Set R,
    AlgebraicIndependent Q (Subtype.val : S -> R) ->
    (forall x in S, Transcendental Q x) -> dimH S = 1.

The English "Every" and Chinese "一切" agree. Neither language restricts to
maximal, infinite, uncountable, measurable, positive-measure, or basis sets.
Both source hypotheses are retained. The counterexample is additionally
nonempty, so it does not depend on admitting the empty set.

DEFINITION CORRESPONDENCE

1. AlgebraicIndependent is the actual Mathlib predicate, defined as injectivity
   of MvPolynomial.aeval. The variable type is S itself, and Subtype.val is the
   inclusion S -> R. Thus the polynomial variables are indexed by actual distinct
   members of the source set. There is no unrelated family or changed domain.
2. Transcendental Q x is the actual Mathlib negation of IsAlgebraic Q x.
   IsAlgebraic means that a nonzero rational-coefficient univariate polynomial
   evaluates to zero. Irrationality is not substituted for transcendence.
3. dimH is the pinned library's Hausdorff dimension, taking values in ENNReal.
   In this Mathlib revision its actual declaration name is the root-level dimH,
   despite documentation and the preassessment referring to MeasureTheory.dimH.
   The inspection log prints its body: the supremum of dimensions at which the
   corresponding Hausdorff measure is infinite. Its argument is the same S as
   in the algebraic hypotheses, under the existing standard real metric.
4. The equality to 1 is exact equality in ENNReal. The dimension theorem is
   exactly 0, and zero_ne_one in that type rules out equality to 1.
5. The only new proposition is HausdorffClause, an abbreviation in mathematical
   content for the displayed universal statement. Its body is printed by the
   inspection harness. There is no replacement definition of independence,
   transcendence, dimension, measure, density, or spectrum.

MATHEMATICS

The set of real numbers algebraic over Q is countable. One standard justification
is that rational polynomials form a countable set and each nonzero polynomial
has finitely many real roots; Mathlib's Algebraic.countable supplies the proved
general result. The real line is uncountable (Cardinal.not_countable_real).
Consequently there exists a real x transcendental over Q.

Take S = {x}. This set is nonempty. For a singleton index type, evaluation on the
multivariable polynomial ring identifies with one-variable polynomial evaluation.
Mathlib's algebraicIndependent_singleton_iff proves that its inclusion is
algebraically independent if and only if x is transcendental. Applied directly
to the subtype {x}, it establishes the exact set-level independence required.
Every member of S equals x and is therefore transcendental over Q.

Every singleton has Hausdorff dimension zero, by dimH_singleton. Thus this S
satisfies both antecedents of the conjecture's first clause, but not its
conclusion. This refutes the universal Hausdorff clause.
