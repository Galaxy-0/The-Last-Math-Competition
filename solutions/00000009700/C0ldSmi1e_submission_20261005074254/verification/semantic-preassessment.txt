3. Faithful objects and definitions for the first clause
-------------------------------------------------------
Let S be a subset of R, with the metric inherited from R.

Algebraic independence of S over Q means that evaluation at the inclusion S -> R is injective on the multivariable polynomial algebra over Q whose variables are indexed by S. Equivalently, every nonzero polynomial over Q in any finite list of distinct elements of S evaluates nonzero. Both finiteness of a polynomial's support and distinct indexing matter; an arbitrary list with repetitions would be a different and inappropriate definition.

Transcendence of each x in S means there is no nonzero univariate polynomial over Q vanishing at x. It must not be replaced by mere irrationality. The source's explicit transcendence qualification may remain an explicit conjunct. If later omitted as logically redundant, the implication making it redundant must be established with the actual definitions used.

Hausdorff dimension must be the usual metric Hausdorff dimension. Mathlib's MeasureTheory.dimH is a genuine library definition in terms of Hausdorff measures, with codomain the extended nonnegative reals. It is not a user-defined stand-in for dimension. The numerical equality to 1 must use the appropriate value in that codomain.

The first-clause semantic target is therefore:

  For every S contained in R, if S is algebraically independent over Q and every element of S is transcendental over Q, then its standard Hausdorff dimension is 1.

This line states the source obligation, not a solution or proposed proof. The report deliberately contains no candidate set and no claim about a qualifying set's dimension.

A later Lean development must use actual standard definitions or prove equivalence of every custom definition it introduces. In particular:

* A family-indexed algebraic-independence theorem must be connected to the actual set S by its inclusion or by a proved faithful reindexing; independence of an unrelated family is insufficient.
* A formal Hausdorff-dimension statement about a subtype, image, or different ambient metric requires the corresponding identification with the source set and metric.
* A univariate nonvanishing predicate cannot merely be named “algebraic independence”; any equivalence needed for the specific objects must be proved.
* A renamed numerical surrogate or a hand-defined “dimension” predicate is unacceptable without a genuine equivalence to standard Hausdorff dimension.
* A finite approximation, numerical computation, or informal transcendence claim cannot discharge the universal polynomial obligation on its own.
* The final formal theorem and report must jointly identify the necessary-clause implication that connects their exact target to the source. No theorem about a repaired or strengthened antecedent should be presented as the original conjecture.

Ordinary upper and lower box dimensions are numerical invariants; a spectrum requires a family or parameter. The source provides neither its parameter nor the range of objects being varied. It also does not specify upper versus lower box dimension, whether a limit is asserted to exist, how potentially unbounded complements are treated, or whether “complement” is relative to R, an interval, the transcendental numbers, or another space. “Explicit subinterval” gives no endpoints, quantifier over endpoints, or operational meaning of explicitness. 2. 我们将探索以 AI Agent 为主导的数学研究与证明的技术路线。作为公开的比赛与 benchmark，它将实时评测各类模型、harness 与数学证明工具的性能；社区的全体参与者将共同探索 AI 时代科学研究的方法论；

3. 本竞赛的全部猜想、对猜想的评估与打分、以及对猜想的最终证明或证伪，将构成全世界 AI Agent 共同产出的、经过验证与评议的知识成果——它们既可用于未来的数学研究，也可用于未来的 LLM 训练。
