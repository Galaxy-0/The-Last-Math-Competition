\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} rainbow-triangle-free edge colorings are
complete-graph colorings without triangles of three distinct colors.
\textit{Conjecture:} the number of rainbow $C_4$'s in such colorings is
at least $(1/24)\cdot n^2$; the extremal configuration is attained by
recursive constructions from balanced 4-partite colorings, and the
constant $1/24$ is optimal.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

\textbf{Step 1: the class contains no rainbow $C_4$ at all.} Let
$v_1v_2v_3v_4$ be a $C_4$ whose cycle edges carry four
pairwise-distinct colors $a,b,c,d$ (edges $v_1v_2, v_2v_3, v_3v_4,
v_4v_1$). The coloring is of a complete graph, so the diagonal
$v_1v_3$ has a color $x$. Triangle $v_1v_2v_3$ is rainbow unless
$x \in \{a,b\}$; triangle $v_1v_3v_4$ is rainbow unless
$x \in \{c,d\}$. Since $\{a,b\} \cap \{c,d\} = \emptyset$, one of the
two triangles is rainbow --- contradiction. Hence the rainbow-$C_4$
count of \emph{every} member of the class, for every $n$, is $0$.

\textbf{Step 2: a concrete exhibit.} The constant 2-coloring
($\mathrm{col}(a,b)=\mathrm{false}$ for all edges) is symmetric, lies
in the class (three edges from two colors always contain an equal
pair, so no rainbow triangle), and has no rainbow $C_4$ (four cycle
edges from two colors cannot be pairwise distinct). The Lean package
exhibits this member for every $n$ and certifies all three properties.

\textbf{Step 3: the bound fails.} With count $0$, the claim
``count $\ge (1/24)n^2$'' clears denominators to $24 \cdot 0 \ge n^2$,
false for every $n \ge 1$ since $n^2 > 0$.

The minimum of the rainbow-$C_4$ count over the class is therefore
$0$ for every $n \ge 1$: no positive lower bound holds, the constant
$1/24$ is invalid (not merely non-optimal), and the
extremal-configuration claim is vacuous.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} checks exhaustively that no 3- or 4-tuple of
Bool values is pairwise distinct, that the constant 2-coloring of
$K_n$ ($4 \le n \le 9$) has zero rainbow $C_4$s and zero rainbow
triangles, that all $864$ $K_4$ colorings carrying a rainbow $C_4$
(cycle edges a permutation of four colors, diagonals arbitrary) force
a rainbow triangle, and that $(1/24)n^2 > 0$ for $1 \le n < 200$
(exact fractions). The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
the pigeonholes, the exhibit member, and the arithmetic; all ten
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

Point 1 is a finite case argument on the $K_4$ spanned by the cycle
(prose); the Lean kernel certifies its pigeonhole cores, the concrete
2-color member of the class, and the clearing-denominators arithmetic.
The lower-bound clause is refuted in full; no position is taken on
variant conjectures about other coloring classes.
