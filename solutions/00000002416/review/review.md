## Semantic audit

The conjecture's first clause states that the spectrum of the minimal number of generators of open subgroups of G_Q is {2, 3}. The submission refutes it exactly — genSpectrum(G_Q) ≠ {2, 3}, and indeed 2 ∉ genSpectrum and 3 ∉ genSpectrum — by proving the much stronger statement that no open subgroup of G_Q is topologically finitely generated: the spectrum is {⊤} (∞).

The mathematics is the classical argument, formalized end-to-end. Step 1: for each prime p, χ_p(σ) = ±1 according as σ(√p) = ±√p is a homomorphism G_Q → ℤˣ (a case check from σ(√p)² = p), with open kernel containing the fixing subgroup of ℚ(√p). Step 2: χ_p = χ_q implies √p·√q is fixed by all of G_Q, hence rational (via Mathlib's `InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup` at the trivial intermediate field), so (pq) is a rational square, hence a natural square, forcing p ∣ q and p = q — contradiction. Step 3: two homomorphisms with open kernels agreeing on a topological generating set are equal (the equalizer contains an open subgroup, hence is closed, hence contains the closure of the generated subgroup = the whole group); so a finite generating set T would inject the infinite set of primes into the finite set {±1}^T. Step 4: if an open subgroup U of the compact group G_Q were topologically finitely generated by S, then adjoining coset representatives yields a finite topologically generating set of G_Q (the closure of ⟨S ∪ R⟩ is a closed subgroup containing U and R, hence every coset). Contradiction. The abstract-generation variant follows since ⟨S⟩ = U implies density in U.

Faithfulness: G_Q is Mathlib's absolute Galois group with the Krull topology; "topologically generates" is density of the generated subgroup; minGen ∈ ℕ∞ is the infimum of finite generating-set sizes, ⊤ when none exists — the standard meaning of "minimal number of generators" for profinite groups; the spectrum is the set of d(U) over open subgroups with subspace topology, which contains d(G_Q) itself. The submission also refutes the abstract (non-topological) reading, so no reading of "generators" rescues the clause. The main theorem states the exact refutation needed (spectrum ≠ {2,3}, 2 and 3 absent), and the remaining two clauses (d = 3 density, Shafarevich completeness) are conjuncts whose truth is moot. The two equal ℚ-algebra structures on `AlgebraicClosure Q` are handled with an explicit local instance priority, so all statements refer to the same group as `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup Q`.

Sanity check: G_Q surjects onto Gal(ℚ(√p)/ℚ) ≅ Z/2 for every prime p — infinitely many distinct quotients of order 2 — while a topologically finitely generated profinite group has only finitely many open subgroups of each index. The conclusion spectrum = {∞} is therefore correct; the conjectured {2, 3} is impossible.
