# Conjecture 00000002594: Disproof

Let P be the ordinal sum of antichains of sizes (1,1,3), and let Q be a two-element chain. Each factor satisfies LYM, but their direct product has rank sizes (1,2,4,3) and a maximal antichain with Lubell sum 1/2+3/4=5/4>1.

## Scope

This refutes unrestricted product closure as stated. The example also addresses the source's reference to maximal antichains. No extra log-concavity hypothesis on rank sizes appears in the source; the additional claims about matching permutations and braiding are not needed for the disproof.
