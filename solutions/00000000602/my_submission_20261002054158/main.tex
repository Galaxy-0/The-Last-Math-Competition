\begin{conjec}[00000000602]
Let $V_K$ denote the Jones polynomial and let $\omega$ be a primitive
fourth root of unity.  For every alternating knot $K$:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $|V_K(\omega)|^2$ divides $\det(K)^2$, and
  \item the quotient $\det(K)^2 / |V_K(\omega)|^2$ equals $|V_K(i)|^2$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{conjec}

\section*{Verdict}

\textbf{FALSE.}  The conjecture already fails at the trefoil, the smallest
alternating knot.

\section*{The attack: $\omega = \pm i$}

The primitive fourth roots of unity are exactly $\omega = \pm i$, whose
complex conjugates are their negatives.  Since the Jones polynomial of a
knot has real coefficients, $V_K(\overline{z}) = \overline{V_K(z)}$, and
therefore
\[
  |V_K(\omega)|^2 = |V_K(\pm i)|^2 = |V_K(i)|^2 .
\]
So clause~(2) collapses to the requirement
\begin{equation}\label{eq:collapse}
  \det(K)^2 = |V_K(\omega)|^2 \cdot |V_K(i)|^2 = |V_K(i)|^4 .
\end{equation}

\section*{Counterexample: $K = 3_1$}

Take the right-handed trefoil $3_1$, which is alternating.  In the standard
normalization $V(\text{unknot}) = 1$,
\[
  V_{3_1}(t) = t + t^3 - t^4 .
\]
This was recomputed from scratch (independent of tables) via the Kauffman
bracket state-sum on the PD code $X(1,4,2,5),\,X(3,6,4,1),\,X(5,2,6,3)$,
which yields $\langle 3_1\rangle = A^{-7} - A^{-3} - A^5$ and, with
$V(t) = (-A)^{-3w}\langle K\rangle|_{A = t^{-1/4}}$, $w = 3$, exactly
$t + t^3 - t^4$; the normalization check $V(1) = 1$ passes.

Evaluating exactly on Gaussian integers:
\[
  V_{3_1}(i) = i + i^3 - i^4 = i - i - 1 = -1, \qquad
  V_{3_1}(-i) = -1 ,
\]
so
\[
  |V_{3_1}(\omega)|^2 = |V_{3_1}(i)|^2 = 1
  \quad\text{for both } \omega = \pm i .
\]
The determinant is $\det(3_1) = |\Delta(-1)| = 3$ with Alexander polynomial
$\Delta(t) = t^{-1} - 1 + t$, hence $\det(3_1)^2 = 9$.  Now:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Clause (1) happens to hold: $1 \mid 9$.
  \item Clause (2) fails: the quotient is $9/1 = 9$, but
        $|V_{3_1}(i)|^2 = 1$, and $9 \neq 1$.
\end{itemize}
Equivalently, the collapsed requirement \eqref{eq:collapse} reads
$9 = 1$, which is absurd.  Hence the conjecture is false.

\section*{Further witnesses}

The same exact evaluation applied to the published Jones polynomials of
other small alternating knots (all recomputed in the accompanying
\texttt{reproduce.py}) shows the failure is not an accident of the
trefoil: in every case $|V_K(i)|^2 = 1$ while $\det(K)^2 > 1$.

\centering
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule

\midrule
$3_1$ & 3 & 9  & 1 & $9 \neq 1$ \\
$4_1$ & 5 & 25 & 1 & $25 \neq 1$ \\
$5_1$ & 5 & 25 & 1 & $25 \neq 1$ \\

\end{table}

\section*{Boundary of the disproof}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Only the \emph{quotient identity} (clause 2) is refuted; the
        divisibility clause (clause 1) is untouched by this counterexample.
  \item Since $|V_K(i)|^2 = 1$ for all alternating knots tested, the
        identity clause would force $\det(K) = 1$; restricting the
        conjecture to high-crossing or high-determinant knots cannot
        repair it.
  \item The numbers $|V(\pm i)|^2 = 1$ are stable under mirroring
        ($V_{\bar K}(t) = V_K(1/t)$) and conjugation, so the chirality
        convention is irrelevant to the disproof.
\end{itemize}
