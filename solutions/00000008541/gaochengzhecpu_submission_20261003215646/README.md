# Conjecture 00000008541: Disproof

The free lattice on two generators consists of x meet y, x, y, and x join y. The proof establishes its universal property into every lattice and shows that every possible length ball contains at most four elements. Its bounded growth contradicts the asserted universal doubly exponential growth law.

## Scope

The source gives no restriction n>=3; the counterexample uses n=2 in the ordinary meet/join lattice signature. The bound is independent of the unspecified length convention. No growth conclusion for free lattices with three or more generators is claimed.
