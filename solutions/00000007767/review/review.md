## Semantic audit

The official statement's leading assertion is an equivalence over the class it defines — commuting polynomial pairs of degree ≥ 2 on P^1: |Per(f) ∩ Per(g)| = ∞ ⟺ ∃ m, n > 0 with f^m = g^n. This is a universally quantified iff, and its forward implication (infinitude forces a common iterate) is exactly the statement that fails: by the classical Julia–Fatou–Ritt theory, commuting polynomials either share an iterate or are, up to conjugacy, common monomials or common Chebyshevs, and in the monomial exceptional family the periodic sets intersect infinitely without any common iterate. The submission instantiates precisely this standard counterexample: f = X^2, g = X^3 over ℂ, acting on P^1(ℂ).

The Lean formalization is faithful at every joint. P^1 is modeled as `Option ℂ` (`some z` = [z:1], `none` = [1:0]) — the standard affine chart, with every projective point having exactly one chart form; a polynomial acts by `Option.map p.eval`, i.e. genuine evaluation on the affine chart with ∞ fixed (`projectiveMap_affine`, `projectiveMap_infinity`), which is the canonical extension of a polynomial to P^1. `Periodic F P := ∃ r > 0, F^[r] P = P` is the standard positive-return-time definition of periodicity (not preperiodicity); `CommonPeriodicSet` is literally Per(f) ∩ Per(g); `CommonIterate F G := ∃ m n > 0, F^[m] = G^[n]` is exact equality of self-maps, the non-vacuous reading (m = n = 0 would trivialize the notion, as the report notes). `LeadingAssertion` quantifies over actual `Polynomial ℂ` objects with `2 ≤ natDegree` and `Function.Commute` of the projective extensions — matching the official hypotheses verbatim — and asserts the iff; `conjecture_false : ¬ LeadingAssertion` is derived from `counterexample`, which bundles all four required facts: degrees 2 and 3 (`powerPolynomial_degree`), commutation (`powerMap_commute`, both composites act as z ↦ z^6 and fix ∞), infinitude of the common periodic set, and nonexistence of any common positive iterate. This is the exact negation of the officially quantified statement, not a strengthening mismatch: the conjecture's hypotheses (commuting, degree ≥ 2) are all satisfied by the exhibited pair and the asserted equivalence is violated.

The mathematics is correct. Iteration satisfies H_d^r([z:1]) = [z^(d^r):1] (`powerMap_iterate_affine`, induction on r). For each k, ζ_k = exp(2πi/5^(k+1)) has exact order 5^(k+1) (`root_primitive`); since gcd(2, 5^(k+1)) = gcd(3, 5^(k+1)) = 1, Euler's theorem gives 2^t ≡ 3^t ≡ 1 (mod 5^(k+1)) for t = φ(5^(k+1)) > 0, so ζ_k^(2^t) = ζ_k = ζ_k^(3^t) — a genuine common periodic point with strictly positive return times (`primitiveRoot_periodic`, `root_periodic_two`, `root_periodic_three`). Distinct k give roots of distinct exact orders, hence distinct points (`root_injective`), yielding an injected copy of ℕ into the common periodic set (`common_periodic_infinite`) — infinitude rests on the affine roots alone, not on ∞. Nonexistence of a common iterate: equality of the self-maps evaluated at [2:1] gives 2^(2^m) = 2^(3^n) in ℂ, transferred to ℕ, injectivity of base-2 powers gives 2^m = 3^n, and parity (m ≥ 1 forces the left side even, the right odd) contradicts it (`no_positive_common_iterate`) — excluding all positive index pairs, not a bounded search. The refutation of the leading equivalence suffices for the whole conjunctive conjecture, and the same counterexample in fact also kills the "both are iterates of a single h" clause (that would imply a common iterate h^(ab)). The report discloses, rather than hides, the two interpretation choices the source leaves open — complex coefficients (the conventional Ritt-theory setting, implied by the mention of Ritt decompositions) and positive iterate indices — and claims nothing about the undefined final uniqueness terminology. My independent exact-arithmetic checks confirm: no solutions of 2^m = 3^n for 1 ≤ m, n ≤ 30; ord(2) = ord(3) = 4·5^(k−1) mod 5^k for k ≤ 5; totient return times 2^φ = 3^φ ≡ 1 mod 5^k; and 2^(2^m) ≠ 2^(3^n) for all 1 ≤ m, n ≤ 12.
