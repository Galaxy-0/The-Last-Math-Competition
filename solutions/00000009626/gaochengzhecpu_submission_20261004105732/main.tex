\begin{abstract}
The unrestricted class of two-dimensional shifts of finite type contains the
full shift on three symbols. Its spatial entropy is $\log 3$, contradicting
the asserted upper bound $\log 2$. We give the complete counting argument
and a Lean 4 formalization of the configurations, finite-type condition,
pattern counts, real logarithms, and entropy limit.
\end{abstract}

\section*{The assertion addressed}
Conjecture 00000009626 asserts, in both its English and Chinese versions,
that the set of realizable spatial entropies of two-dimensional shifts of
finite type (SFTs) is $[0,\log 2]$. Neither version restricts the alphabet to
two symbols. We use the ordinary definition allowing any finite alphabet.
The independent assertions about row-by-row approximation are unnecessary:
the claimed entropy interval is already false.

\section*{An explicit SFT and its entropy}
Let $A=\{0,1,2\}$ and let
\[
                 X=A^{\mathbb Z^2}.
\]
This is the full shift: every configuration is allowed. It is a shift of
finite type defined by the empty finite collection of forbidden patterns.
It is nonempty, closed, and invariant under every lattice translation.

For $n\geq 1$, write $Q_n=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}^2$, and let $N_X(n)$ be the
number of patterns on $Q_n$ occurring in configurations in $X$.
Every map $p:Q_n\to A$ occurs: extend it to $\mathbb Z^2$ by assigning
the symbol $0$ outside $Q_n$. Conversely every occurring pattern is such a
map. Since $|Q_n|=n^2$, independent choices at all sites give exactly
\[
                         N_X(n)=3^{n^2}.
\]
Using the usual square-box definition of spatial entropy (natural logarithms),
\[
 h(X)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log N_X(n)}{|Q_n|}
     =\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log(3^{n^2})}{n^2}
     =\log 3.
\]
The normalized expression is identically $\log 3$ for every positive $n$,
so there is no approximation or unproved limiting interchange in this step.

\begin{proposition}
The entropy spectrum asserted in Conjecture 00000009626 is not $[0,\log 2]$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The SFT $X$ constructed above has entropy $\log 3>\log 2$, because the
natural logarithm is strictly increasing on the positive real numbers.
Thus its entropy lies outside the asserted interval.
\end{proof}

\section*{Scope and formal verification}
This counterexample uses three symbols and addresses the unrestricted
statement that was submitted. Adding a binary-alphabet restriction would
change that statement; the present example makes no claim to disprove an
upper bound for binary subshifts. No classification theorem for SFT
entropies is needed here. For background on the actual unrestricted
spectrum, see Hochman and Meyerovitch~\cite{HM}.

The accompanying \texttt{lean/Main.lean} defines actual configurations
$(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z)\to\operatorname{Fin}(3)$, SFTs through finite
forbidden square patterns at every translate, and occurring patterns through
extension to an actual configuration. It proves an equivalence between all
square patterns and occurring patterns of $X$, then computes their cardinality.
\texttt{HasSpatialEntropy} is the convergence of the normalized real
logarithmic counts along boxes of side $n+1$; this harmless reindexing excludes
the empty box. The main theorem \texttt{counterexample} provides an SFT,
its proved entropy limit, and the strict violation of the proposed bound.
\texttt{not\_universal\_entropy\_bound} negates the bound explicitly.
