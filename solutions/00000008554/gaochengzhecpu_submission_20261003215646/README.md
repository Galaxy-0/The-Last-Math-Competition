# Conjecture 00000008554: Disproof

The rank-two Boolean lattice has an atom/coatom crosscut complex consisting of two isolated vertices. Its ordinary and reduced integral homology are nonzero in degree 0 and zero in every positive degree. Thus its homological dimension is 0, while rank minus one is 1. The formal proof covers all simplices and all homological degrees.

## Scope

Homological dimension is the largest degree of nonzero homology, under either ordinary or reduced homology. The dimension clause fails in both conventions. Ordinary Euler characteristic is 2 while the Mobius number is 1; reduced Euler characteristic is 1, so the manuscript explicitly retains the reduced Euler identity.
