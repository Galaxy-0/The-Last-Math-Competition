  coefficient sequences. Its Noetherian instance is imported mathematics.
  The paper gives the independent elementary least-order proof that all
  nonzero ideals are principal.
- All prime quotients are nontrivial commutative rings. An essential ideal
  must be nonzero because it meets the whole ring nontrivially, and can itself
  serve as the required nonzero two-sided ideal. Right closure is proved.
- The radical is the actual Jacobson construction on the zero ideal. Local
  ring theorems identify it with the maximal ideal generated by X.
- For every N, the true series X^N lies in J^N and has coefficient one at N.
  This proves nonzero powers for all stages; no bounded search is substituted.
- The coefficient at N of every multiple of X^(N+1) is zero. Thus X^N does
  not lie in the next power, establishing strict descent as well.
- The finite indexed infimum of ideals includes its last term and every
  power contains the last term, giving the two inclusions for equality.
- Membership in every radical power kills every coefficient of a series.
  Extensionality proves the infinite intersection is zero. Consequently the
