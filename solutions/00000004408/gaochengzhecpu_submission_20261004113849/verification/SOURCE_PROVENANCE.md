## Reading of the source

Both languages define equivalence by measure-preserving transformation and null modification, then quantify over every equivalence class. Neither restricts the first assertion to already-step graphons. The proof targets this first assertion. It does not replace exact equivalence by approximation, and does not require a definition of jump count. The usual finite-partition interpretation of “step” is enough; the proved obstruction also excludes countably many blocks or, more generally, any essentially countably valued representative.

**English.** Definition: L^p reconstruction equivalence is the equivalence of graphons under measure-preserving transformations and zero-measure modification. Conjecture: Every equivalence class contains a step representative of minimal step count, the step count is an invariant of the class, and on purely jumping graphons it equals exactly the number of jumps. (minimal step-count invariant of L^p classes)
