# Internal semantic review

This records internal scrutiny of the submission, not an official competition review.

Both language versions assert that `10!/10^10` is unimprovable for homogeneous diagonal products at nonzero integer points. The proof uses coordinate-diagonal forms in the given integer lattice. Neither version supplies affine shifts, excludes zero products, or requires every coordinate of a nonzero vector to be nonzero.

1. **Actual objects.** `productAt` evaluates `Matrix.mulVec` on an integer vector cast into the reals and takes the actual finite product of its coordinates. The coefficient matrix is `Matrix.diagonal`; the normalizing determinant is Mathlib's `Matrix.det`.
2. **Full coefficient class.** The main `uniformDiagonalBound` predicate quantifies over every real coefficient vector, including singular matrices. The final optimality predicate uses this unrestricted class. `uniformNonsingularDiagonalBound` separately treats all nonzero diagonal coefficients; `diagonal_det_ne_zero` establishes their actual nonzero determinant.
3. **Admissible witness.** `firstCoordinate` is an actual integer vector `(1,0,...,0)` in every dimension `n+2`. It is proved nonzero by its first coordinate. Its second coordinate vanishes, and the corresponding linear-form factor is zero for every diagonal matrix.
4. **Exact minimum and uniformity.** The absolute product is zero at this witness and nonnegative at every other integer vector. Thus it is a global minimum, not merely an infimum or a sampled small value. The bound holds uniformly for every nonnegative constant. The identity matrix, with determinant one, rules out negative constants in both coefficient classes.
5. **Sharpness and dimension.** The general theorem covers every dimension at least two through `n+2`; dimension ten is the exact specialization `n=8`. The claimed constant is the actual factorial quotient `10!/10^10`, whose positivity is proved. `positive_improvement` supplies the strictly positive and strictly smaller half-constant for the whole class. The final theorem negates the original sharpness conjunct.
6. **Scope and context.** Refuting that conjunct suffices; the separate convex-body clause is not formalized or claimed false. The report accurately distinguishes the repository's unshifted formula from the classical shifted statement on page 2 of Kathuria–Raka, arXiv:2009.09992v1. The administrative PR5 discussion is acknowledged and is not treated as a previous solution.
7. **Trust and independent scrutiny.** Inspection found no admitted proofs, added axioms, unsafe declarations, native evaluation shortcuts, or altered kernel trust settings. Eleven central theorem dependencies are printed by the audit file. An independent agent read the exact bilingual statement, final proof and audit sources, underlying matrix APIs, and matching report, and found no remaining semantic blocker. The submitting agent separately rebuilt and replayed the final sources in a fresh project.
