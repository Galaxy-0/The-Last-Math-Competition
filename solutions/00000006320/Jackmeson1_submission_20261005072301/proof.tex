\section{The conjecture}

Conjecture 00000006320 reads (English version; the Chinese version agrees):

\begin{quote}
\emph{Definition:} The permanent lower bound and the support structure are two layers.
\emph{Conjecture:} There exist two matrix classes with the same lower-bound constant but different
support structures, and the separation is realized by an explicit pair with the same constant but
different support families.
\end{quote}

This is an existence claim. We give an explicit pair of matrix classes, prove that they have the
same permanent lower-bound constant, and prove that their support families are different.

\section{Reading and definitions}

All matrices are real. The permanent of an $n\times n$ matrix is
$\per M=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\prod_i M_{\sigma(i),i}$ (Mathlib's \texttt{Matrix.permanent}).

\begin{definition}
A \emph{matrix class} is a set $\mathcal{C}$ of real $n\times n$ matrices. Its \emph{permanent
lower-bound constant} $c(\mathcal{C})$ is the optimal constant $c$ with $\per M\ge c$ for all
$M\in\mathcal{C}$, that is, $c(\mathcal{C})=\inf\{\per M: M\in\mathcal{C}\}$. This is the form of
a permanent lower bound such as van der Waerden's $\per M\ge n!/n^n$ for doubly stochastic
matrices. We use that bound only as context, never in a proof.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}
The \emph{support} of $M$ is $\supp M=\{(i,j): M_{ij}\neq 0\}$. The \emph{support family} (support
structure) of a class is $\{\supp M: M\in\mathcal{C}\}$.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{The explicit pair.} Let $\Omega_2$ be the set of $2\times2$ doubly stochastic matrices
(nonnegative entries, all row and column sums equal to $1$; Mathlib's \texttt{doublyStochastic}).
Let $\Omega_2^{+}\subseteq\Omega_2$ be the subset of those with all entries positive.

\section{The proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:form}
Every $M\in\Omega_2$ has the form $\begin{pmatrix} a & 1-a\\ 1-a & a\end{pmatrix}$ with
$0\le a\le1$. Hence $\per M=a^2+(1-a)^2\ge\tfrac12$, with equality iff $a=\tfrac12$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The row sums give $M_{01}=1-M_{00}$ and $M_{10}=1-M_{11}$, and the first column sum gives
$M_{10}=1-M_{00}$. So $M_{11}=M_{00}=a$. Nonnegativity of $M_{00}=a$ and $M_{01}=1-a$ gives
$0\le a\le1$. Then
$\per M=M_{00}M_{11}+M_{10}M_{01}=a^2+(1-a)^2=\tfrac12+2(a-\tfrac12)^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[same constant]\label{thm:const}
$c(\Omega_2)=c(\Omega_2^{+})=\tfrac12=2!/2^2$. In both classes, $\tfrac12$ is the \emph{least}
permanent: it is attained at $J_2/2$ (all entries $\tfrac12$), which lies in $\Omega_2^{+}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:form} gives the lower bound on $\Omega_2\supseteq\Omega_2^{+}$, and
$\per(J_2/2)=\tfrac14+\tfrac14=\tfrac12$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[different support families]\label{thm:supp}
The support family of $\Omega_2^{+}$ is $\{\text{full}\}$. The support family of $\Omega_2$ is
$\{\text{diagonal},\ \text{anti-diagonal},\ \text{full}\}$. In particular the identity
$I_2\in\Omega_2$ has diagonal support, which does not occur in $\Omega_2^{+}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Members of $\Omega_2^{+}$ have all entries nonzero. In $\Omega_2$, Lemma~\ref{lem:form} gives
supports anti-diagonal for $a=0$, diagonal for $a=1$, and full for $0<a<1$. All three occur: at
the swap matrix, at $I_2$, and at $J_2/2$.
\end{proof}

Theorems~\ref{thm:const} and~\ref{thm:supp} together give the conjecture: $(\Omega_2,\Omega_2^{+})$ is
an explicit pair of matrix classes with the same constant $\tfrac12$ but different support
families. \qed

\begin{remark}[scope and conventions]
(1) We prove the constant in the strongest sense: an attained minimum (\texttt{IsLeast}) in both
classes, and also as an infimum (\texttt{sInf}). (2) A support is a set of positions, not a set
of positions up to row and column permutations. Even up to permutation, the support families
differ: $\Omega_2$ contains supports with $2$ nonzero entries, while every support in
$\Omega_2^{+}$ has $4$. (3) The example is the case $n=2$ of the van der Waerden setting, with
$\Omega_n^{+}$ dense in $\Omega_n$. We do not use the general van der Waerden theorem. (4) The
reading of ``explicit pair'' is a pair of explicit matrix classes. The pair of explicit matrices
$(J_2/2, I_2)$ is also exhibited: $J_2/2$ attains the common constant in both classes, and $I_2$
realizes a support that only $\Omega_2$ has.
\end{remark}

\section{Lean formalization}

File \texttt{lean/Conjecture6320/Basic.lean} (Lean 4.33.1, Mathlib \texttt{v4.33.1}). It defines
\texttt{lbConst} (infimum of \texttt{Matrix.permanent} over a class), \texttt{supp},
\texttt{suppFamily}, \texttt{Omega2} $:=$ Mathlib's \texttt{doublyStochastic} $\mathbb{R}$
\texttt{(Fin 2)}, and the positive subclass \texttt{Omega2pos}. The theorems are:
\begin{itemize}
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:form}, with $a=M_{00}$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Omega2\_form}: the three entry identities;
\item \texttt{Omega2\_bounds}: $0\le a\le1$;
\item \texttt{permanent\_fin\_two}, \texttt{perm\_eq}: $\per M=a^2+(1-a)^2$;
\item \texttt{perm\_ge\_half}: $\per M\ge\tfrac12$;
\item \texttt{perm\_eq\_half\_iff}: $\per M=\tfrac12$ iff $a=\tfrac12$.
\end{itemize}
\item \texttt{isLeast\_Omega2}, \texttt{isLeast\_Omega2pos}, \texttt{lbConst\_Omega2},
\texttt{lbConst\_Omega2pos}, \texttt{const\_eq\_vdW}: Theorem~\ref{thm:const}.
\item \texttt{suppFamily\_Omega2}, \texttt{suppFamily\_Omega2pos}: Theorem~\ref{thm:supp}.
\item \texttt{separation}: the main theorem. It states \texttt{IsLeast} for both classes, equal
\texttt{lbConst}, \texttt{suppFamily Omega2} $\neq$ \texttt{suppFamily Omega2pos}, and that the
support of $I_2$ lies in the first support family and not in the second.
\end{itemize}
\texttt{lake build} succeeds. \texttt{\#print axioms} for \texttt{separation},
\texttt{suppFamily\_Omega2} and \texttt{const\_eq\_vdW} reports only
\texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}. The code contains no \texttt{sorry} and no
\texttt{native\_decide}.
