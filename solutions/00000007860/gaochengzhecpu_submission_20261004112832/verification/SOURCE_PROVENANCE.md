# 00000007860

**English.** Definition: The waste of randomized greedy coloring: the difference W(G) between the number of colors chi_greedy(G) used by greedy coloring in a uniform random vertex order and chi(G). Conjecture: On G(n,1/2) the waste W(G) concentrates at n/(2 log_2 n)(1+o(1)); and for every graph W(G) <= n/2 - sqrt(n/2) (controlled by the greedy tournament model of local density and chromatic number); asymptotic equality is attained by the balanced correction of complete bipartite graphs (the greedy worst-case of the Turan situation). (greedy coloring waste law)
