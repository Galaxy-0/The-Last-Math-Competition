\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} Sensitivity is always at most $\sqrt{2}\log n$ times
the spectral norm (Gotsman--Linial type sqrt-two law).
\end{quote}

\section{The counterexample (the 1-cube)}

Take $f = \mathrm{NOT}$: $f(0) = 1$, $f(1) = -1$. Its Fourier coefficients
on the 1-cube are
\[
  \hat f(\varnothing) = \tfrac{1 + (-1)}{2} = 0, \qquad
  \hat f(\{1\}) = \tfrac{1\cdot 1 + (-1)\cdot(-1)}{2} = 1,
\]
so $\lVert \hat f \rVert_1 = 1$. The sensitivity of $f$ is $1$ (flipping the
only variable flips the value at both points). The conjectured bound at
$n = 1$ is
\[
  \sqrt{2} \cdot \log(1) \cdot 1 = \sqrt{2} \cdot 0 = 0,
\]
since $\log 1 = 0$ in every base. The claimed inequality is $1 \le 0$:
\textbf{false}, independently of the logarithm base and of the constant
$\sqrt2$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} computes the coefficients, the norm, the sensitivity
and the bound ($0$) on the 1-cube, and enumerates the 2-cube under the
natural logarithm (violations occur there as well). The Lean package (core
Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the six closed statements --- the arithmetic of
$0/2$ and $2/2$, the norm $1$, the sensitivity $1$, the bound $0$, and
$\neg(1 \le 0)$ --- all \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

Only the base-free $n=1$ case is formalized; the $n=2$ enumeration lives in
\texttt{reproduce.py}. The ``pointer maxima'' clause is not addressed.
