## Semantic audit
The conjecture claims a classification: the graphs whose critical group Jac(G) (sandpile group) has zero cyclic factors — glossed in the text itself as "Jac entirely cyclic-free" — are exactly the subdivisions of closed Eulerian graphs. For a connected graph Jac(G) is a finite abelian group, so "zero cyclic factors" is equivalent to Jac(G) = 0, and the claim is the biconditional Jac(G) = 0 ⟺ G is a subdivision of a closed Eulerian graph. The submission refutes the direction "subdivision of closed Eulerian ⇒ Jac = 0" with the infinite family of all cycles C_m, m ≥ 3: each C_m is connected and carries the closed walk `cycleGraph.cycle`, which is a trail using exactly m distinct edges (the handshake lemma gives exactly m edges since every degree is 2), so C_m is a closed Eulerian graph and, by the trivial subdivision, a subdivision of one; C_3 is the smallest closed Eulerian simple graph with an edge, so these are not "trivial exceptions".

The critical group is formalized on the conjecture's own objects in both standard models: Jac = Div⁰/im L with Div⁰ the degree-zero integer vectors and L = D − A Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.lapMatrix ℤ`, and the sandpile group as the cokernel of the reduced Laplacian (row/column of a sink deleted). Nontriviality is obtained by the explicit character φ(x) = Σᵢ i·xᵢ mod m: reindexing shows φ(Lf) = Σⱼ fⱼ(2j − (j+1) − (j−1)) = 0, so φ kills Prin and descends to a surjection Jac(C_m) → ℤ/m (the class of δ₁ − δ₀ maps to 1); the same is proved for the reduced-Laplacian cokernel. Hence Jac(C_m) is nontrivial, and `jac_cycle_has_cyclic_factor` shows every direct-sum decomposition of it has a nontrivial summand, pre-empting any quibble over the meaning of "cyclic factor". The decisive `not_conjecture` is the negation of the ⇐ direction at the counterexample family, and `not_classification` packages the full witness (subdivisible ∧ Jac nontrivial ∧ surjections in both models) for every m ≥ 3.

The mathematics was independently sanity-checked: Jac(C_m) ≅ ℤ/m (m spanning trees by the matrix-tree theorem), in particular nontrivial — and indeed the true classification of graphs with trivial sandpile group is the trees/forests, so the conjecture's stated class is wrong by any account, the cycles being the sharpest obstruction. The report is candid about scope: only the ⇐ direction is formalized (the zero-step subdivision suffices), the isomorphism Jac(C_m) ≅ ℤ/m is left as an unformalized remark, and the alternative reading of "cyclic Z-components" as infinite-cyclic summands is discussed and not pursued. This repairs exactly the vacuity for which PR #267 was rejected (numeral identities with no graph or group in Lean).
