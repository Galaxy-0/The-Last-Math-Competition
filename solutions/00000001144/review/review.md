## Semantic audit

The conjecture is a universal statement: every hyperbolic-type Weyl group of a Kac–Moody algebra has growth rate 2. The submission's decisive theorem `C1144.disproof` ends with `¬ (∀ (n : ℕ) (C : GCM n), IsHyperbolic C → growthRate C = 2)` — the exact negation of that universal claim — established by one explicit, genuine counterexample, so no quantifier manipulation is involved.

The formalization uses the conjecture's own objects, not surrogates. A `GCM` is a generalized Cartan matrix in the standard sense (a_ii = 2, a_ij ≤ 0, a_ij = 0 ↔ a_ji = 0); `IsHyperbolic` is the standard symmetrizable-case definition: indecomposable, A = D·S with D positive integral diagonal and S symmetric and indefinite, and x^T S x ≥ 0 for every x supported on any proper index subset (i.e. every proper principal submatrix PSD — which for symmetric matrices is equivalent to Kac's "every proper connected subdiagram of finite or affine type"). The Weyl group is the subgroup of GL_n(Z) generated by the fundamental reflections r_i(α_j) = α_j − a_ij α_i on the root lattice (the matrices are involutions, proved in Lean), and the growth rate is the limsup of |sphere_k|^{1/k} for the word metric generated by the simple reflections — the standard definition. The witness FF = [[2,−1,0],[−1,2,−2],[0,−2,2]] is the GCM of the Feingold–Frenkel hyperbolic algebra A_1^{++} (over-extension of A_1^{(1)}); its hyperbolicity is proved from scratch in Lean (det = −2 so neither finite nor affine; Q(1,2,2) = −2 < 0 < Q(1,0,0); PSD on every proper principal restriction; connected diagram), and my independent numerical check confirms the determinant, minors, quadratic-form values, r_i² = 1, r_0 r_2 = r_2 r_0, and (r_0 r_1)³ = 1.

The mathematics of the bound is correct. Using only r_i² = 1 and r_0 r_2 = r_2 r_0 (both proved in Lean by `decide` on the explicit matrices), every word reduces to one avoiding `ii` and the factor `20` with no greater length; the resulting subword-counting recurrence F_0' ≤ F_1 + F_2, F_1' ≤ F_0 + F_2, F_2' ≤ F_1 with weights (5, 5, 3)/ (5/3)^m gives #reduced words of length j ≤ 13(5/3)^j, hence |B_k| ≤ 13(k+1)(5/3)^k ≤ (19/10)^k eventually (13(k+1) ≤ (57/50)^k, and (57/50)·(5/3) = 19/10). The `root_bound` lemma then correctly yields: both root sequences eventually ≤ 19/10, both limsups ≤ 19/10, and neither tends to 2. Non-convergence to 2 refutes the claimed value 2 without needing existence of the limits; the limsup versions refute the limsup reading. The report is careful and honest: it states precisely which readings are refuted (universal, limsup/ball/limit) and which are not (existential reading — true; "at most 2"/"supremum 2" — only numerical evidence against), and its numerical remarks (plastic number ≈ 1.3247 as sphere-ratio limit, consistent with W ≅ PGL₂(Z)) are flagged as evidence only.
