# 00000008535

**English.** Definition: The characteristic polynomial of a lattice is the generating function of its Mobius function. Conjecture: The roots of the characteristic polynomial of a geometric lattice are real and negative when the lattice is supersolvable; and in general the minimum of the modulus spectrum of roots is controlled by the Mobius absolute value of its minimal antichain. (real-rootedness and modulus bound of characteristic roots)

**中文。** 定义：格的特征多项式为 Möbius 函数的生成函数。猜想：几何格的特征多项式的根为实负数当格为超可解；一般几何格的根模长谱的最小值由其最小反链的 Möbius 绝对值控制。（特征根实性与模长界）
