## Semantic audit

The conjecture fixes w_2(F) := |K_2(O_F)| and asserts, as its main displayed clause, w_2(F) = |ζ_F(−1)|·D_F·2^{[F:ℚ]} for number fields F (a conjunction with further clauses about a local-dilogarithm p-part and a unit-rank logarithmic law). The submission refutes the displayed equality at F = ℚ — which refutes the conjunction — by observing that the right side evaluates to 1/6: |ζ(−1)| = 1/12, D_ℚ = 1, [ℚ:ℚ] = 1. Since 1/6 is not a natural number, it cannot be the order of any group, so the equality fails regardless of what K_2(ℤ) is. ℚ satisfies every stated hypothesis (the conjecture states no unit-rank or integrality restriction), so this is a genuine counterexample, and the negation formalized is actually stronger than needed: the Lean theorem quantifies over every type G and shows Nat.card G ≠ the right side, covering G = K_2(ℤ) whatever its construction, and for every candidate reading of ζ_ℚ(−1).

The formalization is faithful and unusually careful about the one real trap: Mathlib's `NumberField.dedekindZeta` is the raw Dirichlet/L-series, which diverges at s = −1, so its Lean value there would be junk. The submission never evaluates it there. Instead it proves every ideal of 𝓞_ℚ ≅ ℤ is generated by the natural number equal to its absolute norm (`ideal_eq_span_absNorm`, `absNorm_span_nat`), hence the norm-n coefficient is a_n = 1 (`card_ideals_of_norm`), so the L-series coincides with `riemannZeta` on Re s > 1; then, by the identity theorem on the connected open set ℂ∖{1}, any function holomorphic there agreeing with the series on the half-plane takes the value riemannZeta(−1) = −1/12 at −1 — which is exactly the value ζ_ℚ(−1) of the meromorphic continuation. `riemannZeta_is_continuation` proves the hypotheses are satisfiable, so the main theorem is not vacuous. This is the correct standard way to give meaning to ζ_F(−1), not a loophole.

The mathematics is correct: ζ(−1) = −B₂/2 = −1/12 (Bernoulli B₂ = 1/6, proved in Lean as `riemannZeta_neg_one`), 1/12 · 1 · 2 = 1/6, and no natural number equals 1/6 (`nat_ne_rhsQ`); all verified numerically.
