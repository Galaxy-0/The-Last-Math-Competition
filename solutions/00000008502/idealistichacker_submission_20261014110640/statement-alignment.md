The original is a conjunction: a general dimension upper bound/equality clause,
AND an exact spectrum {1,2,3,4} for equations y''=ay, with every listed value
realized. The exact spectrum necessarily excludes dimension zero. No nonzero-a,
irreducibility, nontrivial-group or proper-PV-extension condition is stated.
The counterexample uses ordinary scalar order-two Picard-Vessiot semantics.

SecondOrderPV exposes a coefficient, actual invertible fundamental matrix,
entrywise companion equation, exact algebraically closed Complex constants

and generated PV field. The identityPV definition supplies every field for
the actual RatFunc Complex instance. A separate actual generated-ring theorem
and ideal theorem show its PV ring is the base field and differentially simple.
The matrix equation has a genuine scalar y''=ay companion interpretation;
its nonzero Wronskian provides independent solutions 1,t.

differentialGaloisGroup is the derivative-commuting subgroup of actual
K-algebra automorphisms of L. PVRepresentation includes a group homomorphism
to actual GL2(Complex) and a correctness field against U^(-1)*sigma(U), after
the constant-field embedding. selfRepresentation has constant value one

the group is trivial. Its represented image is proved to be the identity.

including inverse determinant, and proves the range equals the determinant

galoisDimension uses the Krull dimension of this represented closure's ring.
It is not a preset zero value or a statement about the base field alone.

must hold there. This refutes a necessary clause, without weakening it to an
