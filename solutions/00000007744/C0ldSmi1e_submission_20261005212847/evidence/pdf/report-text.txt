The quadratic trace is deterministic: a disproof of Conjecture 00000007744  C0ldSmi1e 5 October 2026  Abstract  In the uniform simple   d -regular graph model, the squared adjacency matrix has trace  nd   for every graph on   n   vertices. Thus its centered quadratic trace, with any deterministic matrix and trace normalization and with the stated   √ n   factor, is identically zero.   Its law is exactly   δ 0   at every admissible size, and every weak limit has variance zero.   For   d   = 3 , the conjecture instead prescribes variance   8/9 .   We formalize the actual uniform model, polynomial traces, finite-dimensional laws, and the weak-limit contradiction in Lean 4.19.0 with Mathlib v4.19.0.  1   The original claim and the clause refuted  The supplied English statement reads:  Definition:   The spectral empirical measure of random d-regular graphs (uniform model) converges to the Kesten-McKay law   KM d ; the second-order limit is the joint limit process of normalized trace polynomials times   √ n . Conjecture: The limit is Gaussian with covariance  Σ( p, q )   given by   ( d   + 1) -regular constrained counts of noncrossing return path pairings of   p  and   q ; for   p   =   q   =   x 2   one has Var   = 4   −   12/ d   + 8/ d 2 , degenerating to the Wigner value   4   of GUE fluctuations as   d   grows. (Kesten-McKay second-order fluctuations)  The Chinese version makes the centering explicit:  定义： 随机   d - 正则图 （一致模型） 的谱经验测度收敛到   Kesten–McKay   律   KM d ； 其二阶极限 分布指规范化迹多项式   ( tr   p ( A )   −   E )   ·  √ n   的联合极限过程。猜想：该极限为高斯族，协方差 函数   Σ( p, q )   由   p, q   的非交叉返回路径配对的   ( d   + 1) - 正则约束计数给出；特别对   p   =   q   =   x 2 ，  Var   = 4   −   12/ d   + 8/ d 2 ，且当   d   → ∞   时退化为   GUE   波动的   Wigner   值   4 。  Both versions require the displayed quadratic-coordinate variance.   We disprove that explicit requirement at fixed degree   3 . We do not dispute the first-order Kesten–McKay convergence or claim that every other coordinate vanishes. No interpretation of the unspecified general pairing rule is needed. The graph with   ( i, u )   adjacent to   ( j, v )   exactly when   i   =   j   and   u ̸   =   v   is a disjoint union of   k   + 1  copies of   K d +1 , hence is   d -regular. This proves nonemptiness of the sample space at every such size. The random graph is still drawn uniformly from   all   regular graphs on   V d,k ; the block graph is only an existence witness. Lean proves the stated cardinality, its positivity, and   n d,k   → ∞ . Let   a k , b k   be any deterministic real sequences and let   μ k   be the actual law of   Y a k   ,b k   on this model. Proposition 1 gives   μ k   =   δ 0   for every   k , so   μ k   ⇒   δ 0 . If also   μ k   ⇒   ν , uniqueness of weak limits gives   ν   =   δ 0 , and therefore Var ν   ( id ) = 0 .  This argument identifies the entire limiting law.   It does not interchange variance and weak limits. Lean uses the usual weak topology on   ProbabilityMeasure   R , its Hausdorff property, and the ordinary measure-theoretic variance. For   d   = 3   this is an unbounded sequence of admissible sizes   4( k   + 1) . A limit asserted over all admissible sizes must also hold along this sequence. The finite trace identity holds on every finite label set; the product labels impose no additional restriction on the sampled graphs.  4   From the joint polynomial process to the contradiction  For an arbitrary real polynomial   p , the formal project defines  T p ( G ) =   b   Tr ( p ( aA G ) ) ,   Z p ( G ) =   ( T p ( G )   −   E T p  ) √ n  2
