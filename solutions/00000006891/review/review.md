## Semantic audit
The conjecture asserts, as its first conjunct, that the geometric characterization of tensor rank is the hierarchy of secant varieties with the rank spectrum exactly equal to the hierarchy index. Since "secant variety" standardly means the Zariski *closed* object (Landsberg, Harris, Milne), the hierarchy index of first appearance is the border rank, and the assertion becomes rank = border rank pointwise — stated in the source without any genericity qualifier (the "general tensors" wording belongs to the separate dimension-counting clause). The submission refutes this pointwise equality with the classical W tensor and thereby falsifies the conjunction; it correctly does not touch the generic dimension-counting clause, since one false conjunct suffices.

The witness is `W = E100 + E010 + E001` in `Tensor = (Fin 2 → ℂ) ⊗[ℂ] ((Fin 2 → ℂ) ⊗[ℂ] (Fin 2 → ℂ))`, a genuine Mathlib tensor product equipped with a tensor basis and a linear coordinate equivalence (`Core.lean`), so "simple tensor" means actual `a ⊗ₜ (b ⊗ₜ c)` and rank means the minimum number of such summands (`RankLE`/`tensorRank` via `Nat.find`). Rank 3 is proved by an exact elimination with no division: writing the two-slice equations for a hypothetical two-term decomposition, the identities δ·b0·c1 = d1, δ·b1·c0 = d1, δ·b1·c1 = 0 (δ = a0·d1 − a1·d0) give d1² = (δb0c1)(δb1c0) = (δb0c0)(δb1c1) = 0, so d1 = 0, and symmetry gives a1 = 0, whence the 100-coordinate of the second slice would be 0 ≠ 1 — contradiction. This is the classical rank-3 fact for the W state, correctly formalized, and degenerate factors/zero summands are handled by padding rather than division.

The projective geometry is developed honestly rather than axiomatized: projective vanishing of homogeneous polynomials is defined representative-independently and proved equivalent to evaluation on any representative via the scaling identity p(λz) = λ^d p(z) (derived from the monomial expansion); the projective Zariski closure is the common zero locus of *all* homogeneous polynomials vanishing on the set, proved to be the least algebraic superset (monotone, idempotent, empty-set correct via the constant 1); secant varieties are closures of genuine projective spans of Segre points, with Mathlib's `Projectivization.Subspace.span` connected to vector submodule spans. Two results make the formalization airtight against reading-shifting: `mk_mem_secantSpanLocus_iff_rankLE` proves the *unclosed* span locus is exactly the rank-≤r locus (so the unclosed reading would make the conjecture's first clause a tautology, not a theorem about geometry), and the decisive facts place `[W]` in σ₂ via the explicit polynomial arc on honest secant lines (t ≠ 0) plus continuity of polynomial evaluation over ℂ (using density of ℂ∖{0}), while excluding it from σ₁ by the explicit flattening minor that vanishes on the Segre but takes value −1 on W.
