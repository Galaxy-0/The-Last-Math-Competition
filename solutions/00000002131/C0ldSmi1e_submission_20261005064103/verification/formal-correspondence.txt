MATHEMATICAL DERIVATION

Use one-based permutation notation in this prose. Let p=q=1, the only permutation
of length one. These are permitted: the source supplies no minimum length beyond
being ordinary nonempty patterns, and the semantic preassessment expressly
includes positive length one.

For each n>=1, every permutation of {1,...,n} contains the pattern 1 by selecting
one position. Consequently a_n(1)=0 for every positive n. Taking the genuine real
nth roots gives a constant zero sequence and L(1)=0.

The direct sum p direct-sum q is 12: the first block has value 1 and the second
block has value 2. A permutation s contains 12 precisely when there are two
positions i<j with s(i)<s(j). Thus s avoids 12 precisely when i<j implies
s(i)>s(j): it is strictly decreasing. Since s is a bijection onto the same
finite ordered set, it is the unique reverse permutation n,n-1,...,1.
Therefore a_n(12)=1 for every n>=0, and the genuine positive-index real nth-root
sequence is constantly 1. Hence L(12)=1.

All three limits in the asserted equality therefore exist, and
L(1 direct-sum 1)=L(12)=1 != 0=L(1)*L(1).
The universal displayed equality is false. Any conjunction asserting this
equality and an additional characterization clause is consequently false,
independently of the content of that additional clause. This argument does not
assert that the unspecified clause is independently false.
