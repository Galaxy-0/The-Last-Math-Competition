Cardinal.{u} consists of cardinalities of Type u objects, via a quotient by actual equivalences; Cardinal.mk is that quotient map (SetTheory/Cardinal/Defs.lean:83,89). It is not a natural-number cardinality and should not be replaced by Nat.card or Fintype.card for infinite objects. G and each subgroup subtype naturally lie in the same universe; κ can then be Cardinal.{u}. A quantification over all such carriers lives in a higher sort but can produce a Prop. The theorem should be universe-polymorphic unless the author explicitly supplies a faithful reduction to the chosen universe.

If groups, thresholds, or indexing objects are put in different universes, use Cardinal.lift with explicit compatible levels and prove the transport required by comparisons and equalities. Cardinal.lift is defined via ULift (SetTheory/Cardinal/Defs.lean:151). Do not compare incompatible cardinal universes by accidental coercion or use a type universe itself as a group cardinal.

The cardinal ℵ_ω is Cardinal.aleph Ordinal.omega0 in the relevant universe; ℵ_1 is Cardinal.aleph 1. Cardinal.aleph enumerates infinite cardinals by ordinal indices (SetTheory/Cardinal/Aleph.lean:348); Ordinal.omega0 is the first infinite ordinal (SetTheory/Ordinal/Basic.lean:734).
