## Semantic audit
The official conjecture asserts that for the parallel-thickened uniform matroid U_{r,n}^{(k)}, the Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial coefficients are rational with **strictly alternating signs** (the "in particular" clause of the proposed hypergeometric formula). The submission's counterexample is the parallel double of U_{3,4}: ground set {0,1,2,3}×{0,1}, independence = injectivity of the class map with at most 3 classes — in Lean, the actual `Matroid.comap Prod.fst` of a uniform matroid built from the independence axioms (`IndepMatroid.ofFinite`), so it is a genuine matroid by construction, proved loopless, of rank 3 on 8 elements, with each parallel class a certified 2-element circuit (`thickened34_parallel_pair`). Since any parallel thickening leaves the flat lattice unchanged up to rank-preserving order isomorphism (`comapFlatOrderIso`, proved for an arbitrary surjective class map), the counterexample is robust to every reading of "adding k parallel copies" (one added copy, or two total copies, per element).

The flat lattice is the ranked 12-element lattice: bottom, four atoms (the classes), six rank-2 flats (unions of two classes), top. The Möbius values from the bottom are 1, −1 (×4), +1 (×6), −3 (top), giving characteristic polynomial t³ − 4t² + 6t − 3; the tabulated Möbius function is proved equal to Mathlib's `IncidenceAlgebra.mu` (`mobius_eq_incidence_mu`), not assumed. The KL family is defined by the standard EPW axioms (`IsKLFamily`): diagonal value 1; the strict degree bound deg P < d/2 expressed coefficient-wise as vanishing for all i with 2i ≥ d; and the recursion t^d P_{F,G}(t^{-1}) = Σ_{F≤H≤G} χ_{F,H}(t)·P_{H,G}(t), with reflection at the interval rank implementing t^d p(t^{-1}) exactly under the degree bound. The candidate family (1 + 2t on the rank-3 interval, 1 elsewhere) is verified against every axiom on every interval by kernel-checked finite computation; uniqueness of any such family is proved abstractly by strong induction on interval rank (`klFamily_unique`, the difference of two families is a low-degree polynomial equal to its own high-degree reflection, hence zero); and the final `klPolynomial` is selected from the proved ∃! rather than postulated (`klPolynomial_eq : klPolynomial = 1 + 2*X`). This is a faithful and unusually careful formalization of the EPW definition (Elias–Proudfoot–Wakefield, Adv. Math. 299 (2016), Thm 2.2), including the interval form.

I verified the mathematics twice. By hand: the recursion at the top interval reads t³(a+bt^{-1}) = (a+bt) + 4(t−1) + 6(t²−2t+1) + (t³−4t²+6t−3) = t³ + 2t² + (b−2)t + (a−1), forcing a = 1, b = 2; the proper intervals (rank-2 with 2 or 3 atoms, rank-1) all admit KL value 1, consistent with their recurrences 1 + m(t−1) + (t²−mt+m−1) = t². And independently in python3: a from-scratch implementation of the Möbius function and the EPW recursion over the same 12-element lattice, solving each interval's linear system exactly over ℚ, returns [1, 2] for the bottom-to-top KL polynomial and confirms μ(top) = −3 and the 12 flats. The two adjacent nonzero coefficients 1 and 2 are both positive, so their product is positive. The Lean predicate `AdjacentSignsAlternate` (every adjacent pair of nonzero coefficients has negative product) is precisely the necessary form of "strictly alternating signs" — agnostic to starting sign and reading direction, and correct in the presence of zero coefficients — and `conjecture_false` negates it for the actual polynomial, refuting the official sign clause and thereby the conjunctive conjecture. The hypergeometric-formula clause, whose H-terms the official text leaves unspecified, is not formalized, correctly so; a formula entailing a false sign pattern cannot hold as stated.
