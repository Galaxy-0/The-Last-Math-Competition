# 00000003483

**English.** Definition: Edge counts of four-critical graphs: the Kostochka–Yancey theorem gives the (5v−2)/3 upper bound. Conjecture: Equality is attained only by nearly-four-regular doubly critical graphs, with the secondary edge-count distribution the discrete lattice 5v/3 minus a constant; the lattice spacing corresponds one-to-one with the folding structure of critical subgraphs. (four-critical edge count second level)

**中文。** 定义：四临界图的边数：Kostochka–Yancey 定理给出 (5v−2)/3 上界。猜想：等号仅由近四度双临界图取得且次级边数分布为 5v/3 减常数的离散格；格点间距与临界子图的折叠结构一一对应。（四临界边数第二级）

## Interpretation and scope

Double-critical uses its standard definition: deleting the endpoints of every edge lowers chromatic number by two. Neither language of this source gives a conflicting definition. The necessary double-critical clause already fails, regardless of the undefined nearly-four-regular condition.
