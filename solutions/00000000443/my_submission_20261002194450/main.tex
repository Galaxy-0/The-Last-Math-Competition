\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} For $k = 2$ and all $n \ge 2$,
$\dim L_n \ge 2^{n-1} - 2^{\lceil n/2 \rceil}$, and for $n$ prime the gap
between this bound and the exact value is exactly half of $2^{(n-1)/2}$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

At $n = 5$ (prime), Witt's formula --- the conjecture's own definition ---
gives
\[
  \dim L_5 = \frac{2^5 - 2}{5} = \frac{30}{5} = 6,
\]
while the conjectured lower bound is $2^4 - 2^{\lceil 5/2 \rceil} = 16 - 8
= 8$. The claimed inequality is $6 \ge 8$: \textbf{false}, at a prime, so
the prime-gap clause collapses with the bound. (\texttt{reproduce.py}
lists all violations for $n \le 12$.)

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} evaluates Witt's formula exactly for $n = 2..12$
(hardcoded M\"obius table) and compares with the bound. The Lean package
(core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the four closed statements ($2^5 - 2 =
30$, $30/5 = 6$, $16 - 8 = 8$, $\neg(6 \ge 8)$), all \texttt{does not
depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The lower-bound clause is refuted; the gap clause is moot once the bound
fails.
