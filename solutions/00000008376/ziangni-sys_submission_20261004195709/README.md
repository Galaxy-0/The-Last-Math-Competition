# Counterexample to 00000008376

The standard tropical hyperplane given by the actual max-plus polynomial X0+X1+X2 has central cell {(c,c,c)}. Under the coordinate-permutation S3 action, the origin has actual stabilizer S3, of order six, exceeding the claimed largest stabilizer S2, of order two. The whole central cell is also fixed setwise. After quotienting by diagonal translations, the central projective vertex still has stabilizer S3.

The report explicitly uses the unrestricted largest-stabilizer clause: neither language version excludes central or lower-dimensional cells. It does not address variants restricted to noncentral/maximal-dimensional cells, or the separate McKay-graph, orbit-count and Young-subgroup claims.

Lean defines an actual MvPolynomial over Tropical (WithTop (OrderDual Real)), proves its maximum evaluation, defines the corner locus and its exact monomial-attainment cells, verifies the coordinate action and translation invariance, and computes actual MulAction.stabilizer subgroups. It constructs the genuine quotient by diagonal translations, verifies the induced action, and proves the central cell projects to a singleton whose stabilizer again has order six.
