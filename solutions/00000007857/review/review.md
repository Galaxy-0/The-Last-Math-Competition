## Semantic audit
The conjecture's first clause asserts an asymptotic Θ-identity. A Θ-claim entails an eventual positive lower bound, so refuting it requires exhibiting, in the asserted regime, an instance where no positive constant works — with the failure occurring on a non-negligible (here: full-measure) event, since the claim is made "with high probability". The submission takes p = 1, which the text does not exclude and which lies in the stipulated dense regime (p ≫ log n/n holds because log n/n → 0, proved as `dense_regime` from Mathlib's little-o of log). At p = 1 the Erdős–Rényi model is degenerate: G(N,1) = K_N almost surely. The Lean realizes this honestly rather than by fiat: a one-point probability space carries edge random variables that are proved mutually independent (`iIndepFun`) with pushforward law exactly `PMF.bernoulli 1` (a point mass at true), and `graph_complete_almost_surely` holds almost surely. All spectral work is then deterministic on the complete graph: the adjacency action (Ax)_i = Σ_j x_j − x_i is proved entrywise from Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.adjMatrix` of ⊤; the scaled Laplacian c·(I − A/(N−1)) is shown to act by the scalar cN/(N−1) on the sum-zero subspace (witness e₁ − e₂) with kernel exactly the constants, and `nonzero_eigenvalue_unique` proves every nonzero eigenvalue equals cN/(N−1), so the positive-eigenvalue set is the singleton {cN/(N−1)} and the gap, defined as its sInf, is exactly cN/(N−1) — c = 1 gives the normalized-Laplacian gap ≤ 2 and c = 1/2 the lazy gap ≤ 1, both conventions used in the literature being covered. The lazy matrix P = ½I + A/(2(N−1)) is verified entrywise nonnegative with unit row sums (`lazy_stochastic`), a genuine transition matrix, and I − P = ½L exactly.

On the other side of the Θ, the displayed scale at p = 1 is max(1 − λ₂/N, N·1⁻¹) with the literal np⁻¹ = N/p = N term.
