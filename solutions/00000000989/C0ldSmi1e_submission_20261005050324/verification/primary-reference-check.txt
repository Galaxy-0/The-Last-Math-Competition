Alexander Müller-Hermes, Decomposable Pauli diagonal maps and Tensor Squares of Qubit Maps, arXiv:2006.14543v1 (2020).
https://arxiv.org/html/2006.14543#S1
Sections1 and2, introduction definitional paragraphs and notation paragraph.
The matrix spaces are complex square matrices. Positivity preserves positive-semidefinite matrices; complete positivity requires all finite identity ampliations to preserve that property. Decomposition is a sum of a CP map and output transpose composed with another CP map. No nonzero requirement for the summands occurs. The transpose is the ordinary matrix transpose in a fixed basis. The unnormalized Choi convention is (identity tensor map) applied to the maximally entangled matrix.

Mark Girard et al., On the mixed-unitary rank of quantum channels, author manuscript (2020).
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/Papers/MixedUnitaryRank.pdf
Section1, printed page2, equations1–3 and the following text; section2, printed page5, Choi and Kraus representations.
The Choi rank is the ordinary rank of the matrix constructed from images of matrix units; this paper uses the output tensor factor first. For a map from n-by-n to m-by-m matrices the Choi matrix has side length nm. Its discussion distinguishes CP maps from channels, for which trace preservation is an additional condition.
