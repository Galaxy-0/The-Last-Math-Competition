Conjecture 00000000545: author semantic report

Exact source
English: Definition: Markov bases (quadratic-quadratic). Conjecture: The toric
ideal of the graph ring of K_n has an entirely quadratic Markov basis if and
only if n <= 5 (a criterion beyond Hosten-Sullivant).
The supplied Chinese text states the same equivalence. The source SHA256 is
e960ffafdb92929a3b0a8ef81e196511847a3ab8bafe92b2b965a6845e35d37e.

Result and scope
The conjecture is false under the standard edge-ring meaning of graph ring.
For EVERY finite complete graph, and EVERY nontrivial commutative coefficient
ring (in particular every field), its toric ideal has a finite generating
set of nonzero pure binomials whose two monomials both have degree exactly 2.
Taking n=6 contradicts the asserted necessity n<=5. The counterexample does
not depend on a boundary convention at n=0 or n=1.

Original objects and their exact Lean realizations
1. K_n: vertices are Fin n. CompleteGraph.Edge V is the subtype of Finset V
   with cardinality 2. It therefore represents ALL unordered pairs of
   distinct vertices, without loops, orientations, or missing edges.
   CompleteGraph.edge constructs {a,b}, and edge_rep proves that every edge
   has this form. No custom restricted graph has replaced K_n.
2. Graph ring: CompleteGraph.graphRing V k is the range of graphRingMap V k,
   from the standard mathlib MvPolynomial (Edge V) k into MvPolynomial V k.
   graphRingMap_edge proves X_{a,b} maps to X_a * X_b. The bridge's
   toricMap_eq_eval2Hom (spelled eval₂Hom in Lean) also identifies this map
   with standard polynomial substitution. Thus the range is exactly the
   usual subring generated over k by x_a x_b, a distinct from b.
3. Toric ideal: CompleteGraph.toricIdeal V k is literally RingHom.ker of
   that standard graphRingMap. It is not a relation, numerical surrogate,
   tested finite collection, or separately assumed abstract ideal.
4. Monomials: edgeMonomial V k M is MvPolynomial.monomial M.toFinsupp 1.
   Multiset.toFinsupp is mathlib's additive equivalence between arbitrary
   multisets and arbitrary natural exponent vectors. Arbitrarily large
   edge multiplicities and monomial degrees are included.
5. Degree fiber: degree V M is the multiset of labeled endpoints, obtained
   by joining the two endpoint labels of every edge occurrence. Equality
   means equal degrees AT EACH LABELED VERTEX, not just the same unordered
   multiset of numerical degrees. exponentMap_toFinsupp and
   graphRingMap_edgeMonomial identify this with the actual image exponent.
6. Markov basis: HasQuadraticMarkovBasis V k expands to existence of a finite
   subset of the standard polynomial ring whose ideal span equals the
   actual toric ideal, all members being nonzero pure binomials with
   distinct exponent vectors, each monomial of degree 2, and polynomial
   totalDegree exactly 2. This is the algebraic definition of a quadratic
   Markov basis. The additional arbitrary-fiber connectivity theorem
   connected_of_degree_eq supplies the combinatorial Markov interpretation.
7. The basis used: quadraticBinomials is the set of ALL differences of
   distinct two-edge multisets having equal labeled endpoint multisets.
   Reversed signs or redundant generators are allowed, as in the standard
   notion of a Markov basis; no minimality is asserted or needed.

Complete mathematical proof
Let d(M) be the multiset of endpoint labels of an edge multiset M. Its
cardinality is twice the number of edges. For every vertex v, its count in
d(M) is at most the number of edges, since each edge is loopless.

We first show that whenever d(M)=d({a,b}+Q), with a different from b,
quadratic switches transform M to {a,b}+R for some R.
Since a appears in d(M), remove one occurrence of an edge {a,c}. If c=b,
the desired edge is already present. Otherwise b is not on that removed
edge, so an edge {b,d} remains and can also be removed, leaving S.
If c differs from d, replace {a,c},{b,d} by {a,b},{c,d}. All four edges
are loopless and the labeled endpoint multiset is unchanged.

If c=d, denote the common vertex by c. Then c differs from both a and b.
The count of c in d(M) is 2 plus its count in d(S), whereas the count of c
in d({a,b}+Q) is its count in d(Q). Equal total endpoint cardinalities give
|Q|=|S|+1. Since the c-count in d(Q) is at most |Q|, the c-count in d(S)
is strictly less than |S|. Therefore S contains an edge {x,y} avoiding c.
Orient its two labels so x differs from a; at least one endpoint does.
Remove that occurrence from S, leaving T. Perform
  {a,c},{x,y} -> {a,x},{c,y},
  {a,x},{b,c} -> {a,b},{x,c}.
Each displayed edge is loopless, the rest of the multiset is carried along,
and both steps preserve labeled endpoint counts. If a step is degenerate
it is a reflexive relation and contributes the zero polynomial, not a
zero element in the final basis. All occurrence removals and contexts are
explicit in Multiset.exists_cons_of_mem and SwitchEquiv.add.

Now induct on the number of edges of a target multiset N. Insert one edge
of N into M by the preceding argument, cancel its two endpoint labels from
the degree equality, and apply induction to the remainders. This proves
connected_of_degree_eq for ALL M,N with d(M)=d(N), without any bound on
the number of edges or their multiplicities. SwitchEquiv is the inductive
congruence generated by the actual degree-preserving two-edge replacements;
it has only reflexivity, transitivity, addition of a common multiset, and
those replacements as constructors.

Let J be the ideal spanned by the nonzero quadratic differences. A single
replacement is in J by definition, a reflexive step gives zero, composing
steps adds their binomials, and adding a common edge multiset multiplies
their binomial by the corresponding monomial. The formal lemma
SwitchEquiv.binomial_mem establishes these identities. Hence every
equal-image monomial difference, in every degree, belongs to J.

For completeness the full arbitrary-polynomial kernel step is proved in
IdealBridge, not assumed from a citation. In the quotient by J, any two
monomials with the same image exponent have equal classes. Choose one
class for each realized image exponent. For an arbitrary finite polynomial,
its quotient class is the sum of its coefficients times these classes,
grouped by image exponent. A polynomial in the monomial-map kernel has
every grouped coefficient sum zero, and consequently its quotient class
is zero. Thus the full kernel is contained in J. Conversely every chosen
quadratic generator maps to zero. This proves exact ideal equality.

The set of two-edge multisets is finite for finite V (it is Sym (Edge V) 2),
so the set of their differences is finite. Distinct exponent vectors give
distinct coefficient-one monomials over every nontrivial commutative ring.
Each selected difference is nonzero and homogeneous of degree 2; mathlib's
IsHomogeneous.totalDegree gives total degree exactly 2. These facts give
hasQuadraticMarkovBasis for every finite V and every such k. Finally
counterexample_six combines the n=6 instance with the checked fact 6>5,
and conjecture545_false negates the original universal equivalence.
