## Semantic audit
The conjecture asserts a biconditional: the toric ideal of the graph ring of K_n has an entirely quadratic Markov basis if and only if n ≤ 5. The submission proves the "only if" direction false by proving the stronger theorem that the ideal is quadratically generated for every finite complete graph, so the biconditional fails at n = 6 (P(6) holds while 6 ≤ 5 fails). This is the exact negation of the printed claim, in both the unrestricted and the positive-integer domain; the direction of the refutation is correct and matches the classical fact that the toric ideal of the second hypersimplex I_{2,n} is generated by quadrics for all n.

The formalization is faithful. The graph ring is the standard edge ring: the parametrization sends the generator of each unordered pair {i, j} to x_i·x_j (`graphRingMap_edge`), the toric ideal is the actual `RingHom.ker`, and a Markov basis is a finite binomial generating set; `HasQuadraticMarkovBasis` requires a finite set of pure binomials x^a − x^b with both exponent vectors of total degree two and polynomial total degree exactly two, spanning the kernel — no weakening. Nonvanishing of the generators uses 1 ≠ 0 in k, so the result holds over every nontrivial commutative ring, in particular every field.

The mathematical core is genuinely proved, not asserted. Fiber connectivity (`connected_of_degree_eq`) handles edge multisets with arbitrary multiplicity: the insertion lemma (`insert_edge`) shows that any multiset with the degree of edge e + Q can be moved to e + T' by at most two quadratic 2-switches, including the subtle c = d parity case resolved by a counting argument (`count_degree_le_card`, `exists_avoiding`); induction then connects the whole fiber. The kernel lemma (`ker_monomialMap_le_of_fiber_binomials`) upgrades fiber-connected binomials to the full polynomial kernel with no degree restriction and no binomial hypothesis on the target polynomial, valid over rings with zero divisors. I independently verified the algebra: (x²+y³)-style checks are irrelevant here, but the switch relations, degree preservation, and the span equality were checked line by line, and the claimed K_6 counterexample follows immediately.
