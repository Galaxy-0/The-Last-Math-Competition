# Conjecture 00000000469: disproof

Author: [GodBlf](https://github.com/GodBlf). Submitted October 4, 2026.

Embed a triangle `G = C₃` in the plane. Its dual `H` has two vertices
joined by three parallel edges. The standard nowhere-zero flow and tension
polynomials satisfy `F_G(q) = T_H(q) = q - 1`; their common real root is `1`.
In general plane duality identifies these polynomials, so it cannot imply
disjoint root sets.

## Formal scope

`Graph` stores the actual edge endpoints of the triangle and its dual.
`components` counts the equivalence classes generated by the selected edges.
`flow` and `tension` are the standard subset expansions over the real numbers.
The project proves that both evaluate to zero at `1`, and formally negates
the root-separation assertion for these graphs. It does not merely assume
the answer `q - 1` as the definition of either polynomial.

It also verifies connectivity, the triangle's explicit six-dart rotation
system, the two face cycles and their exhaustion, the spherical Euler count,
and the dual endpoint maps. The report gives the elementary geometric plane
embedding and the correspondence between subset expansions and group counts.
The surface classification theorem and the general counting correspondence
are not formalized in Lean. No `sorry`, `native_decide`, or additional axioms
are used; only the standard Mathlib axioms occur in the audit.

## 中文摘要

三角形的平面对偶是两点之间有三条平行边的图。三角形的循环流必须在三条边上
取同一非零值，对偶图的张力也必须在三条边上取同一非零势差，因此二者都等于
`q - 1`，有共同实根 `q = 1`。报告还说明对偶恒等式为何直接否定根分离，并覆盖
交换两个图、同图比较以及要求两侧都是简单图时的解释。

Lean 使用实际图的连通分量和标准子集展开证明共同实根；另核验旋转系统、面圈、
连通性和对偶端点。Python 独立计算四个多项式的精确系数，全部通过。
