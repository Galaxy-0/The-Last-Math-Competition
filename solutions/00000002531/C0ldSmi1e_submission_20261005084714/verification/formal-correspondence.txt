2. Independent mathematical argument

Take the standard real line with d(x,y) = |x-y|, set k = 1, and take E = R.
Let omega_1 denote Lebesgue measure of the open unit ball (-1,1). Then omega_1
= 2. Standard normalized H^1 is omega_1/2^1 times the raw diameter-cover
Hausdorff measure. The coefficient equals 1, and on R the raw H^1 equals
Lebesgue length. Thus normalized H^1 equals length too. This identifies both
the measure and the normalization; it is not an assumption that every Mathlib
Hausdorff normalization agrees with the chosen convention in all dimensions.

For every x in R and EVERY positive real r,
  H^1(E intersect B(x,r)) = H^1((x-r,x+r)) = 2r.
The unchanged source quotient therefore equals (2r)/r^1 = 2. The full
right-hand limit is 2 at every x, so uniqueness of limits rules out any limit
equal to 0 or 1. This is a failure of the binary-value claim; the limit itself
exists everywhere in this example.

If an H^1-null set N made the binary assertion true at every x in E minus N,
then no point of R could lie outside N. Thus N = R, contradicting
H^1(N) = 0 because H^1(R) = infinity. The argument therefore retains the full
null-set quantifier, rather than exhibiting only an isolated bad point.

E = R is Borel and Caratheodory measurable. Its total measure is infinite,
which is permitted by the source. No global finiteness, local finiteness,
rectifiability, or other additional premise is inserted in the source. The
example's ball measures are finite, as separately proved in Lean, and every
positive-radius denominator is positive and finite. No ENNReal.toReal or
default-valued limit is used anywhere.

4. Source-to-theorem logic and limits of the conclusion

Under the stated conventional reading, the source must apply to the Euclidean
case n=k=1 and the Borel (hence H^1-measurable) set E=R. Both natural a.e.
interpretations in the source, ambient a.e. and restricted-to-E a.e., imply
NullExceptionalBinaryDensity for this E. The finite values 0 and 1 in
HasBinaryDensity are exactly the source's allowed values, and the denominator
is unchanged. Therefore the original compound conjecture implies the necessary
clause BorelDensityClause (and, on the restricted interpretation with the
specified measurable convention, CaratheodoryDensityClause). Lean proves its
negation. Hence the compound conjecture is false under these conventions.

This is a one-way specialization used for disproof, not an equivalence between
the source and a replacement definition. No definition named Conjecture is
introduced that erases the source's later assertions. Those assertions about
nonexistence-exception dimension and tightness are conjunctive; refuting the
earlier necessary binary-density assertion suffices to refute the conjunction.
This proof does NOT independently establish or refute the later assertions,
nor does it identify an exceptional set of nonexistence with a set where an
existing density is not binary. In this example the limit exists everywhere.

The source's unspecified full ambient class, dimensional range, general
normalization, exceptional dimension convention, and sharpness quantifiers
remain unspecified. We make no claim to have uniquely formalized that entire
underspecified text. This disproof applies under the conventional elaboration
approved by the semantic gate. If someone silently divided H^1 by 2, or changed
the source denominator to 2r, this particular conclusion would change; neither
modification is made here. The alternative raw diameter convention in dimension
one gives exactly the same counterexample because the proved coefficient is 1.
