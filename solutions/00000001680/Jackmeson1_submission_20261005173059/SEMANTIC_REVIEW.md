- Minor qualification to the Poisson extension: for spatial intensity n on a region D of area a, the point count has mean an. The claim that P(N >= n/2) tends to one requires a > 1/2; it is false for arbitrary small positive-area regions. It holds for the unit square and unit disk mentioned in the report. For other regions, normalize n to the expected point count or adjust the count threshold and constants. This does not affect the deterministic theorem or the binomial disproof.
- Minor qualification to the literature remark: the quoted formula tw = Theta(r sqrt(n)) cannot hold for unrestricted r(n) > c2, since treewidth is at most n-1. An upper restriction such as r = O(sqrt(n)), or a saturated expression involving min(n, r sqrt(n)), is necessary when stating that regime. The application at r asymptotic to sqrt(log n) is within the unsaturated regime, and the submitted proof does not depend on the citation.
