## Semantic audit
The conjecture says: for `d | p−1`, the minimal `k` with `kH = F_p`, where `H` is the subgroup of `d`-th powers in `F_p^*`, equals `(1+o(1))·log p/(log p − log d)` as `d → ∞` under `d < p^{1−ε}`. The formalization is faithful on every object: `H p d` is Mathlib's range of the `d`-power endomorphism of `(ZMod p)ˣ` viewed in `ZMod p` (exactly the subgroup of `d`-th powers); the `k`-fold sumset is pointwise `k • S` (sums of exactly `k` elements, with an explicit membership equivalence); `coverNumber` is `sInf {k ≥ 1 | k • H = univ}`; `Asymp ε K F` is precisely the uniform `(1+o(1))` statement — for every `η > 0` there is `D` such that `|K/F − 1| < η` for all primes `p` and divisors `d | p−1` with `d ≥ D` and `d < p^{1−ε}` — and `Conjecture165` quantifies over every fixed `ε ∈ (0,1)`. The decisive theorem `C165.not_conjecture165 : ¬ Conjecture165` is the exact negation, no hypotheses strengthened, nothing redefined.

The counterexample is genuine and satisfies every stated hypothesis. For `d = 2(N+2)` (arbitrarily large) Dirichlet's theorem supplies a prime `p ≡ d+1 (mod 2d)` with `p > d^d`, so `p − 1 = d(2q+1)` with odd quotient. If `u^d = −1` then `1 = u^{p−1} = (u^d)^{2q+1} = −1`, a contradiction; hence `−1 ∉ H`, so `0 ∉ H` and `0 ∉ H+H` (from `u^d + v^d = 0` one gets `(vu^{-1})^d = −1`). Thus `K(p,d) ≥ 3`. A cover does exist (`1, 2^d ∈ H` are distinct since `2^d < p`, and Cauchy–Davenport iterated gives `pH = F_p`), so the sInf is a true minimum, not an artifact of an empty set. Meanwhile `d^d < p` forces `4 log d < log p`, i.e. `1 < L ≤ 4/3`, and `d ≥ 1/(1−ε)` gives `d < p^{1−ε}`. So `K/L ≥ 9/4` while the asymptotic requires the ratio to tend to 1 — and since `d ≥ N` for arbitrary `N`, every `D` is violated. The quantifier structure of the refutation matches the statement exactly; the submission additionally refutes robust variants (K against ⌈L⌉, ⌊L⌋, round L, and the "at most k summands" reading via `H ∪ {0}` against L, ⌊L⌋, round L) and explicitly declares which readings it does not refute (K₀ vs ⌈L⌉; readings adding a `d ≥ p^δ` lower bound), which is the honest scope.
