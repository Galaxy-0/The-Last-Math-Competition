\paragraph{Source and interpretation.}
Conjecture \texttt{00000000425} (original statement and pinned commit in
\texttt{SOURCE.md}) asserts that the
coefficient sequence in the diagonal specialization of the boxed plane
partition generating function is always log-concave. Write $F_n(q)$ for the
standard volume generating polynomial of the $n\times n\times n$ box,
so the three box dimensions are specialized to the diagonal.
We disprove both log-concavity of the numerical coefficients of a fixed
$F_n$ and coefficientwise log-concavity of the polynomial sequence $(F_n)$.
Both arguments are formalized in the accompanying Lean file.

\paragraph{Definitions.}
For the box of side two, a plane partition is an array
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ with entries in
$\{0,1,2\}$, weakly decreasing along both rows and columns:
$a\ge b$, $a\ge c$, $b\ge d$, and $c\ge d$.
Its volume is $a+b+c+d$. Let $c_k$ count such arrays of volume $k$.
Log-concavity requires $c_k^2\ge c_{k-1}c_{k+1}$ at every interior index.

\paragraph{Counterexample and proof.}
There is exactly one array of volume zero, namely the zero array, and exactly
one of volume one, with a single $1$ in the top-left entry. The three arrays
of volume two are
\[
\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
These are exhaustive: either one entry equals $2$, which by monotonicity
must be the top-left entry, or two entries equal $1$, giving exactly the
two displayed order ideals. Consequently
\[
c_0=1,\qquad c_1=1,\qquad c_2=3,\qquad
c_1^2=1<3=c_0c_2.
\]
Thus the asserted universal log-concavity is false. It is unnecessary to
consider the additional equality characterization after this first clause
has failed.

\paragraph{Independent polynomial check.}
The complete polynomial, obtained both by direct enumeration and by the
MacMahon product, is
\[
\prod_{i,j,k=1}^{2}\frac{1-q^{i+j+k-1}}{1-q^{i+j+k-2}}
=1+q+3q^2+3q^3+4q^4+3q^5+3q^6+q^7+q^8.
\]
The accompanying Python verifier independently computes the product as an
exact integer formal power series and enumerates the arrays.

\paragraph{Direct formal connection to the MacMahon product.}
Write its numerator and denominator as $N(q)$ and $D(q)$. For the cubic box
of side two, the exact triple products give
\[
 D(q)=1-q-3q^2+O(q^3),\qquad N(q)=1-q^2+O(q^3).
\]
Since $D(0)=1$, formal division defines a coefficient sequence $f$ with
$D(q)F(q)=N(q)$. Comparing the first three coefficients gives
$f_0=1$, $f_1-f_0=0$, and $f_2-f_1-3f_0=-1$; thus again
$(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(1,1,3)$. This directly refutes log-concavity for the product
specified in the conjecture, without using the counting formula as an
unproved assumption.

The self-contained Lean 4.19.0 file \texttt{lean/Main.lean} formalizes both routes.
It verifies the complete, duplicate-free enumeration of the 81 arrays and
the precise plane-partition predicate. It also defines integer formal series,
Cauchy multiplication, and the actual three nested MacMahon products. The
terminating recursion \texttt{formalQuotient} constructs every coefficient;
\texttt{formalQuotient\_spec} proves $D F=N$ at every index whenever $D(0)=1$.
The theorem \texttt{macmahon\_product\_counterexample} therefore refutes
log-concavity for an explicitly constructed infinite coefficient sequence,
not for a predicate whose existence is merely assumed. Its first three
coefficients are also proved equal to the direct enumeration above.
All calculations use kernel proofs, with no placeholders or custom axioms.

\paragraph{Coefficientwise log-concavity across box sizes.}
For a sequence of polynomials, coefficientwise log-concavity requires every
coefficient of $F_n(q)^2-F_{n-1}(q)F_{n+1}(q)$ to be nonnegative.
This reading fails at $n=2$ as well. The completely filled box is the unique
term of maximum volume, so $F_n$ is monic of degree $n^3$.
Consequently $F_2^2$ has degree $16$ while $F_1F_3$ is monic of degree $28$,
and
\[
 [q^{28}]\bigl(F_2(q)^2-F_1(q)F_3(q)\bigr)=0-1=-1.
\]
In particular, the claimed nonnegativity fails before any equality
characterization is considered.

\paragraph{Formal verification of the cross-size argument.}
The Lean proof does not assume the preceding degree or monicity facts.
It constructs three actual infinite formal quotients from the exact
MacMahon numerators and denominators for side lengths $1$, $2$, and $3$.
Their full equations $D_nF_n=N_n$ hold at every index by
\path{formalQuotient_spec}; in each case $D_n(0)=1$ is verified.

To calculate efficiently, the file represents the first $29$ coefficients
by finite integer lists. The theorem \path{prefixProduct_correct} proves
for arbitrary lists of factors that the truncated product agrees with the
infinite Cauchy product at every index below the cutoff. Successive kernel
checks compute the actual MacMahon factors, rather than assume their
product or a claimed polynomial table. Three further kernel checks verify
$D_n C_n=N_n$ through degree $28$ for the displayed coefficient lists $C_n$.
The theorem \path{equation_prefix_unique}, proved by strong induction,
then identifies those lists with the first $29$ coefficients of any true
MacMahon quotient. This uniqueness step is valid because $D_n(0)=1$.

Finally \path{macmahon_cross_size_coefficient} applies to arbitrary series
satisfying the three MacMahon equations and proves the displayed
coefficient to be $-1$. The theorem
\path{concrete_cross_size_coefficient} specializes it to the constructed
quotients, so there is no existence assumption left open. The theorem
\path{macmahon_diagonal_coefficientwise_counterexample} negates the
universal coefficientwise assertion using its $n=2$ instance.
Thus both readings described at the start have full formal disproofs.

The independent script \texttt{verify.py} concerns only Conjecture 425.
It enumerates the $2\times2$ arrays, computes the three exact MacMahon
series through degree $28$, compares them with the Lean coefficient
tables, and checks the cross-size convolution. Only the standard Lean
foundational axioms occur; no additional axiom or external decision
oracle is used.
