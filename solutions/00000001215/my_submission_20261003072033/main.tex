\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Only finitely many outerplanar graphs attain cop number $2$.}
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The odd cycles $C_5, C_7, C_9, C_{11}, \dots$ form an infinite family
of outerplanar graphs with cop number exactly $2$.

Every cycle is outerplanar (all vertices on the outer face), and every
cycle of length $\ge 4$ has cop number exactly $2$ (Aigner--Fromme).
The single-cop insufficiency is certified in the Lean package as a
\emph{universal} game-theoretic theorem: on any graph $(V, \mathrm{adj})$
with a robber strategy $\rho$ satisfying the cycle-safety-response
property --- from a Safe position (robber neither on nor adjacent to
the cop), after any legal cop move to a non-capturing position $c'$,
the response $\rho(r, c')$ restores safety --- a \emph{single cop never
captures}: for every cop play there is a robber play that is Safe in
every round. The proof is an induction on the round number; the
non-capture side condition at each step follows from the previous
safety (a cop landing on the robber would have had to move onto a
vertex that was neither the robber nor adjacent to it). Cycles of
length $\ge 4$ satisfy the response property with $\rho$ = ``step away
from the cop''.

Combined with outerplanarity of cycles and the classical upper bound
$c(C_n) \le 2$, the odd cycles give infinitely many outerplanar graphs
with cop number exactly $2$: the finiteness claim is false.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} runs exact retrograde-attractor game searches on
$C_4, \dots, C_{11}$ (state spaces $n^2$ resp.\ $n^3$): cop number
exactly $2$ in every case, with the one-cop insufficiency holding
through $C_{21}$. The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
the universal survival theorem; both audited theorems report
\texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the universal game core; the cycle instantiation
(the step-away strategy), outerplanarity of cycles, and the classical
upper bound are cited in prose with exact script confirmation of the
instances.
