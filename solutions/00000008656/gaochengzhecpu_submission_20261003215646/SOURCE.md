# 00000008656

**English.** Definition: Whitehead minimization: minimizing word length under automorphisms. Conjecture: The minimal number of moves of the Whitehead algorithm (worst-case steps of minimization) is polynomial (a polynomial step law); the uniqueness of the minimized word (unique minimal representative) holds within the orbit (an orbit uniqueness law); the Whitehead graph gives a complete criterion (a complete criterion law); and the extension to multivariate (vector) cases has polynomial complexity (a polynomial extension law). (Whitehead polynomial orbit uniqueness graph criterion)

**中文。** 定义：Whitehead 极小化：自同构作用下字长的极小化。猜想：Whitehead 算法的最小移动（极小化的最坏步数）为多项式（多项式步数律）；且极小化字的唯一性（唯一极小代表）的唯一在 orbit 内（轨道唯一律）；Whitehead 图（图判据）的完全刻画（完全判据律）；判据的扩展（多变量（向量）的扩展）的扩展复杂度为多项式（多项式扩展律）。
