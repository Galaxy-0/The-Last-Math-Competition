\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} Every $n$-vertex graph of minimum degree at least
$3$ contains $\Omega(\log n / \log\log n)$ cycles of pairwise distinct
prime lengths.
\end{quote}

\section{The counterexample family}

Let $G_k$ be the disjoint union of $k$ copies of $K_{3,3}$, so $n = 6k$.
For every $k \ge 1$:

\textbf{Minimum degree $3$.} $G_k$ is $3$-regular: each vertex is
adjacent to the three vertices on the other side of its copy of
$K_{3,3}$.

\textbf{Bipartite.} Color each vertex by its side within its copy;
every edge joins opposite colors.

\textbf{All cycles have even length.} Traversing a cycle flips the
color at each of its $p$ steps and returns to the start; hence $p$ is
even.

\textbf{No prime cycle lengths.} A cycle has $p \ge 3$, and the only
even prime is $2$; so $G_k$ has exactly $0$ cycles of prime length,
hence $0$ distinct prime cycle lengths, for every $k$.

\textbf{Unboundedness.} $n = 6k \to \infty$ while the count stays $0$;
since $\log n / \log\log n \to \infty$, any $\Omega(\log n /
\log\log n)$ lower bound would demand a positive count for all large
$n$. The conjecture fails.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} builds $K_{3,3}$ and its disjoint unions from
scratch, checks $3$-regularity and bipartiteness (BFS $2$-coloring),
exhaustively enumerates all simple cycles of $K_{3,3}$ (lengths exactly
$\{4,6\}$), and confirms the count of distinct prime cycle lengths is
$0$ for $k = 1,\dots,6$.

The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1, no Mathlib) certifies,
uniformly in $k$: the $3$-regularity; the color-flip edge lemma
(endpoint colors sum to $1$); the even-steps induction; the parity
dichotomy; and the main theorem that no cycle of prime length exists in
$G_k$ for every $k$, together with the unbounded-family statement. All
nine theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms} under
\texttt{\#print axioms}. The only non-kernel step is the standard
reduction from ``count $=0$ on an unbounded family'' to the failure of
an $\Omega(\cdot)$ lower bound, stated in prose.

\section{Boundary}

Only the displayed universal statement is refuted; variants (minimum
degree $\ge 4$, counting all even cycle lengths, \dots) are not
addressed.
