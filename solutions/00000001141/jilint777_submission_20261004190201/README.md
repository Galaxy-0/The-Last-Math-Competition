# Disproof of conjecture 00000001141

The conjecture is a conjunction:

1. **The Krull dimension of the centre of the restricted enveloping algebra `u(sl_n)` is `n − 1`.**
2. For every reductive `g`, the centre of `u(g)` is spanned by the Weyl-invariant polynomial ring
   of the p-centre, with dimension equal to the rank.

Clause 1 is false for every `n ≥ 2` and every prime `p`, so the conjecture is false. Clause 2
also fails for `g = sl_2`, but the disproof does not need it.

## Why clause 1 fails

- By Jacobson's PBW theorem, `dim u(g) = p^(dim g)`, so `u(sl_n)` is finite-dimensional.
- Its centre `Z` is therefore a finite-dimensional commutative algebra. If `P` is prime, `Z/P` is
  a finite-dimensional domain, hence a field. So every prime ideal is maximal and there is no
  chain `P ⊊ Q`.
- Hence `Krull dim Z(u(sl_n)) = 0`, while the conjecture claims `n − 1 ≥ 1`.

Concretely, take `n = 2` and `p = 3`:

- `u(sl_2)` has dimension 27.
- Its centre is 4-dimensional with 81 elements. It has basis `1`, `h+h²+ef`, `2h+2h²+2ehf+e²f²`
  and `2e²hf²+e²h²f²`, and `Z ≅ F_3 × F_3[x,y]/(x,y)²`.
- It has exactly 2 prime ideals, both maximal. So its Krull dimension is 0, not 1.

## Readings covered

| reading | Krull dim of the centre | claim `n − 1` |
|---|---|---|
| `u(sl_n)` over any field of characteristic `p` (literal) | `0` (finite-dimensional algebra) | false for all `n ≥ 2` |
| "dimension" read as `F_p`-dimension, `n = 2` | `dim Z(u(sl_2)) = 5, 4, 7` for `p = 2, 3, 5` | `≠ 1` |
| full `U(sl_n)` in characteristic `p` (report only) | `n² − 1` (Z is finite over the p-centre `F_p[x^p − x^[p]]`; Zassenhaus) | false for `n ≥ 2` |
| `U(sl_n)` in characteristic 0 (report only) | `n − 1` (Harish-Chandra) | **true**, but this is not the stated conjecture: "restricted enveloping algebra" and "p-centre" exist only in characteristic `p` |
| Harish-Chandra part `U(sl_n)^G ≅ S(𝔥)^W` in characteristic `p` (Veldkamp; report only) | `n − 1` | **true**, but it is neither the centre of `u(sl_n)` nor the full centre of `U(sl_n)` (Krull dim `n² − 1`) |

Clause 2 for `g = sl_2` (reductive, rank 1):

- In `u(g)` the p-centre generators `x^p − x^[p]` are 0, so the p-centre only gives the scalars.
- The centre contains the non-scalar Casimir element.
- Its dimension is 4 (at least 2 in Lean) and its Krull dimension is 0. Neither equals 1.

## Contents

- `report.tex`, `report.pdf`: the complete proof, the explicit `p = 3` computation, and the
  discussion of each reading.
- `lean4/`: a Lean 4.19.0 project that uses the core library only (no Mathlib). It builds in
  under a minute.
- `verify.py` (Python 3 standard library) is an independent check by a different method. It
  rebuilds `u(sl_2)` for `p = 2, 3, 5` by **word rewriting** with the defining relations and
  checks:
  - associativity, the relations, and the PBW basis;
  - the centre, by Gaussian elimination over `F_p`;
  - all prime ideals of the centre (brute force over all subspaces for `p = 2, 3`; nilradical
    and Frobenius for `p = 5`), with no inclusions between them;
  - that the p-centre is central in the full `U(sl_2)`;
  - that the operator tables used in Lean match.
- `verification.txt`: a fresh `lake build` log, the axiom report, the forbidden-token scan, and
  the output of `verify.py`.

## Lean

Everything is defined from scratch.

- `F3` and `V = F_3^27` (basis `bv (9a+3b+c)` ↔ PBW monomial `e^a h^b f^c`).
- `opE, opH, opF`: left multiplication by `e, h, f`. `opRE, opRH, opRF`: right multiplication.
  All are sparse tables.
- `Gen`: the subalgebra `A ⊆ End(V)` generated by `opE, opH, opF`. This is the model of `u(sl_2)`.
- `relations`: `[h,e] = 2e`, `[h,f] = −2f`, `[e,f] = h`, `e³ = 0`, `f³ = 0`, `h³ = h`.
- `ev_bijective`: `φ ↦ φ(1)` is a bijection `A → F_3^27`, so `|A| = 3^27 = p^(dim sl_2)`.
  The step from here to `A ≅ u(sl_2)` uses PBW spanning plus the universal property and is in
  the report, not in Lean.
- `CRing`, `IsIdeal`, `IsPrime`, `HasChain k` (a chain `P₀ ⊊ … ⊊ P_k` of primes), `KrullDimIs d`
  (the supremum of chain lengths is `d`), `Finite`.
- `primes_incomparable`, `finite_krullDim_zero`, `clause_fails_for_finite_rings`: in a finite
  commutative ring every prime is maximal, so its Krull dimension is never `n − 1` for `n ≥ 2`.
- `ZU` / `ZRing`: the centre of `A` as a commutative ring. `ZRing_finite` shows it is finite.
  `P0_prime` gives an explicit prime ideal (the kernel of the trivial character).

Main theorems:

```lean
theorem krullDim_center : ZRing.KrullDimIs 0
def Clause (n : Nat) : Prop := ZRing.KrullDimIs (n - 1)
theorem conjecture_00000001141_false : ¬ Clause 2
theorem krullDim_center_ne : ∀ d, 1 ≤ d → ¬ ZRing.KrullDimIs d
theorem center_not_rank_dim :
    ¬ ∃ z : ZU, ∀ y : ZU, ∃ c : F3, ∀ v, y.val v = vsmul c (z.val v)
```

Axioms: `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice` (the last one from `by_cases` in the
pigeonhole lemma). There is no `sorry` and no `native_decide`. The finite checks use
`decide +kernel`.

Limitations:

- Lean treats `p = 3` and `n = 2` concretely. The statement for all `n` and `p` is the general
  finite-ring lemma (`clause_fails_for_finite_rings`) together with the textbook fact
  `dim u(sl_n) = p^(n²−1)`.
- The exact dimension 4 of the centre is computed in `verify.py`; Lean proves only that it is
  at least 2.

## 中文说明

猜想由两部分合取而成：(1) 限制包络代数 `u(sl_n)` 的中心的 Krull 维数为 `n − 1`；(2) 对一切约化
李代数 `g`，`u(g)` 的中心由 p-中心的 Weyl 不变多项式环张成，且维数等于秩。

**第 (1) 部分对一切 `n ≥ 2` 和一切素数 `p` 都不成立**，因此整个猜想不成立。理由如下：

- 由 Jacobson 的 PBW 定理，`dim u(g) = p^(dim g)`，所以 `u(sl_n)` 是有限维代数。
- 于是其中心是有限维交换代数。对任一素理想 `P`，`Z/P` 是有限维整环，因而是域。所以每个素理想
  都是极大理想，不存在素理想链 `P ⊊ Q`。
- 故 Krull 维数为 `0`，而猜想断言它等于 `n − 1 ≥ 1`。

具体例子取 `n = 2`、`p = 3`：

- `u(sl_2)` 的维数为 27。
- 其中心是 4 维的，共 81 个元素，`Z ≅ F_3 × F_3[x,y]/(x,y)²`。
- 中心恰有两个素理想，且都是极大理想，所以 Krull 维数为 0，不是 1。

第 (2) 部分对 `g = sl_2`（约化，秩为 1）同样不成立：

- 在 `u(g)` 中 p-中心的生成元 `x^p − x^[p]` 都等于 0，p-中心只给出标量。
- 中心含有非标量的 Casimir 元。
- 中心的维数为 4（Lean 中证明了至少为 2），Krull 维数为 0，两者都不等于 1。

本猜想讨论的是"限制"包络代数，它只在特征 `p` 下有意义。我们也讨论了其他读法：

- 若误指特征 `p` 下的完整包络代数 `U(sl_n)`：其中心在 p-中心上有限，Krull 维数为
  `n² − 1 ≠ n − 1`（仅在报告中论证）。
- 若把"维数"理解为线性维数：`dim Z(u(sl_2)) = 5, 4, 7`（对应 `p = 2, 3, 5`），都不等于 1。
- 特征 0 时 `U(sl_n)` 的中心的 Krull 维数确为 `n − 1`（Harish-Chandra）。这是一个**正确**的
  命题，但它不是题目所述的猜想。
- 特征 p 时 `U(sl_n)` 中心的 Harish-Chandra 部分 `U(sl_n)^G ≅ S(𝔥)^W`（Veldkamp）的 Krull 维数为 `n − 1`。这也是正确的命题，但它既不是 `u(sl_n)` 的中心，也不是 `U(sl_n)` 的整个中心（后者 Krull 维数为 `n² − 1`）。

**Lean 形式化**：只用 Lean 4.19.0 核心库，不依赖 Mathlib，所有对象从零定义。

- 在 `F_3^27` 上用三个显式线性算子生成代数 `A`，并验证 `u(sl_2)` 的全部定义关系。
- 证明在 `1` 处求值是双射，所以 `|A| = 3^27`。
- 定义交换环、素理想、素理想链和 Krull 维数。证明有限交换环的 Krull 维数为 0。
- 得到 `krullDim_center : ZRing.KrullDimIs 0` 和 `conjecture_00000001141_false : ¬ Clause 2`。
- 另外证明中心不是一维的（`center_not_rank_dim`）。

**独立验证**：`verify.py`（仅用 Python 标准库）用词重写的方法，对 `p = 2, 3, 5` 重新构造
`u(sl_2)`，计算中心及其全部素理想，并核对 Lean 中使用的算子表。
