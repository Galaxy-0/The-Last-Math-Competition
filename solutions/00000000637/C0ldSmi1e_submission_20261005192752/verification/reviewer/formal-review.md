## Semantic verdict

The modeled object is exactly the standard algebraic definition of the ordinary three-strand braid group, with `Bool` labeling the two Artin generators, and sole relator `(aba)(bab)⁻¹`. There are no distant-generator relations at three strands to omit. `PresentedGroup` quotients the free group by the normal closure of this relator; no additional relations are imposed.

The standard permutation map is constructed through the presentation's proved universal property with images `(0 1)` and `(1 2)`. Its relator check is kernel-checked `decide`. Its kernel is exactly the pure three-strand group, with only a harmless shift from strand labels 1–3 to 0–2. No claim such as `P₃ ≅ ℤ × F₂` is assumed or needed.

The source keeps the standard distinction `Δ = aba` and `T = (ab)³ = Δ²`. It compares the actual center with the subgroup generated by `T²`, the literal **square of the full twist**. The center comes from Mathlib's universal commutation predicate and `Subgroup.zpowers` from the range of all integer powers. These definitions do not encode the conclusion.

The proof establishes `T ∈ P₃`, proves `T` commutes with the braid generators and hence all braids, and restricts this fact to `Z(P₃)`. It constructs the exponent-sum homomorphism to the multiplicative version of `ℤ`, checking the relator rather than assuming a map. The map sends `T` to 6 and `(T²)^k` to `12k`. The integral equation `12k = 6` is impossible. Thus `T ∈ Z(P₃)` but `T ∉ ⟨T²⟩`, so the required center equality fails.

Three lies in the specified range. Since this center equality is a necessary conjunct of the universal original claim, that claim is false. The final theorem states `¬(center equality ∧ Q)` for arbitrary `Q : Prop`; it is correctly presented as conjunction logic. It does not purport to define scl or to establish an scl theorem. This disproof is independent of the omitted scl-domain and uniformity conventions. The eventual report must preserve this precise scope and may not call it a disproof of a corrected half-twist claim.
