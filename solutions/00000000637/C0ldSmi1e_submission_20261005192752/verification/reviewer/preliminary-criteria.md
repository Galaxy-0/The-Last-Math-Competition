## Exact bilingual source claim

English:

> Definition: The center Z(P_n) of the pure braid group. Conjecture: For n ≥ 3, Z(P_n) is generated by the square of the full twist, and the stable commutator length of P_n/Z is bounded (combining the center with scl).

The two versions agree: the domain is strand number at least three; the center-generation assertion and bounded-scl assertion are conjuncts. Neither language authorizes deletion of the word “square,” replacing a full twist by a half twist, adding restrictions on strands/elements, or silently changing what boundedness means. The parenthetical “combining the center with scl” supplies motivation, not an extra mathematical predicate.

## Objects and quantifiers

1. The integer parameter is a finite natural strand count `n`, with `3 ≤ n`. There is no finite upper cutoff. The ordinary reading is universal over such `n`.
2. `B_n` is the ordinary Artin braid group of the plane/disk, with generators `σ_i` for `1 ≤ i < n`, adjacent braid relations, and distant commutation relations. Equivalent geometric/configuration-space definitions are acceptable if their identification is justified. Spherical, surface, virtual, welded, homotopy, or finite braid quotients are different objects.
3. `P_n` is the pure braid group: the kernel of the standard permutation homomorphism `B_n → S_n`, sending `σ_i` to the adjacent transposition. Equivalently it is the fundamental group of the ordered configuration space. An arbitrary group merely named “PureBraid” does not suffice.
4. `Z(P_n)` is the actual subgroup of `P_n` consisting of all elements commuting with every element of `P_n`. Quantification over all pure braids is essential. It must not be defined as the cyclic subgroup being conjectured to equal it.
5. Write `T_n` for the ordinary full twist of all `n` strands. Under the standard Garside convention `Δ_n` is a half twist and `T_n = Δ_n²`. Thus the source's “square of the full twist” means `T_n² = Δ_n⁴`. A consistent inverse convention for positive/negative twisting is harmless for a generated cyclic subgroup, but a half/full confusion changes the claim.
6. “Generated by” means equality with the cyclic subgroup of all *integer* powers, `Z(P_n) = ⟨T_n²⟩`. It is not just centrality, containment of the generator, equality of cardinalities, or the submonoid of nonnegative powers. The full twist and its square must be shown to be elements of the correctly modeled pure braid group.
7. In `P_n/Z`, the letter `Z` naturally refers back to the actual center `Z(P_n)`. The quotient is the group quotient by that normal subgroup, not an abelianization or an independently selected cyclic quotient.
8. For a group `G`, commutator length of `g ∈ [G,G]` is the minimum number of commutators in `G` with product `g`; scl is the limit of `cl(g^k)/k` as positive `k` tends to infinity. An infimum presentation needs its equivalence justified. Commutators must be allowed from all of `G`, and normalization must be stated. The ordinary extension gives `scl(g) = scl(g^k)/k` if a positive power enters `[G,G]`, and infinity if none does. [Calegari, §2.1](https://math.uchicago.edu/~dannyc/books/scl/scl.pdf).
9. Boundedness requires a single finite upper bound for the stated domain of group elements, not merely a finite value for each element or a bound depending on that element.

For the usual restriction of scl to the commutator subgroup and the usual fixed-`n` reading, define `C(n)` as item 6 and `B(n)` as `∃ M : ℝ, ∀ g ∈ [P_n/Z(P_n), P_n/Z(P_n)], scl(g) ≤ M`. The complete claim then has logical shape `∀ n : ℕ, 3 ≤ n → (C(n) ∧ B(n))`. Requiring `0 ≤ M` makes no substantive change.
