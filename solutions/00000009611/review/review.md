## Semantic audit
The conjecture asserts (C1) that the minimal size m(λ) of a nonnegative-integer matrix realizing a Perron number λ as the entropy of a mixing SFT is an explicit function f(d(λ)) of the algebraic degree, and (C2) that f(d)=d iff the coefficients of the minimal polynomial of λ are sign-alternating. The submission reads (C2) per-λ (m(λ)=d(λ) iff sign-alternating), which is the only sensible reading since the right-hand side depends on λ, and states this reading explicitly in both the English report and the Lean preamble.

The Lean formalization is faithful to the official definition. `Realizable lam n` means: n ≥ 1 and there is a primitive (some positive power strictly positive — exactly the mixing condition for the SFT) n×n matrix over ℕ whose Perron eigenvalue is lam, coded as "exists an entrywise-positive eigenvector with eigenvalue lam". The file proves this coding correct rather than assuming it: `norm_le_of_isPerronEigenvalue` shows such λ is the spectral radius (dominates all complex eigenvalue moduli), and `hasEntropy_log` proves the entropy lim (1/k) log N_k equals log λ where N_k is the number of length-k allowed words (`pathCount`). `IsMinSize lam m` is the exact minimum (realizable in size m and no smaller). `algDegree` is `natDegree (minpoly ℚ lam)`; `IsPerronNumber` is integral over ℤ, ≥ 1, and all other conjugates strictly smaller in modulus — the standard definition. `ClaimedDegreeLaw` is exactly (C1) with the official universal quantifier over Perron numbers, and `conjecture_false` is its negation. A second variant `conjecture_false_realized` quantifies over all realized numbers (independent of the Lind theorem and of the Perron-number definition), making the refutation robust to definitional quibbles.

The mathematics is correct and I reverified it independently. Both cubics are irreducible over ℚ (no rational root; the report checks ±1 and ±1,±2,±4). x ∈ [5/4,2] and y ∈ [9/5,2] exist by IVT. The companion-type matrix A3 = [[0,1,0],[0,0,1],[1,1,0]] has eigenvector (1,x,x²) for eigenvalue x (using x³=x+1) and A3⁵ > 0 (numerically [[1,1,1],[1,2,1],[1,2,2]], matching); so m(x) = 3, since a degree-3 algebraic number cannot be an eigenvalue of a smaller matrix (minpoly | charpoly, proved in Lean as `algDegree_le`). A4 = [[0,0,1,2],[1,0,2,0],[0,1,0,0],[1,0,0,0]] is primitive (A4⁵ positive, numerically confirmed), has characteristic polynomial (X−1)(X³+X²−3X−4) (numerically confirmed: X⁴−4X²−X+4) and eigenvector (y, y³−2y, y²−2, 1) — I verified all four row identities by hand using y³ = −y²+3y+4 and y⁴ = 4y²+y−4. For the lower bound m(y) ≥ 4, if y were an eigenvalue of a 3×3 nonneg integer matrix, its charpoly would equal p2 (monic, degree 3, divisible by minpoly), forcing trace = −1, impossible; this does not even use primitivity, so the bound is stronger than needed. Both numbers are Perron (complex conjugate roots have |μ|² = x²−1 < x² resp. y²+y−3 < y²; discriminants negative — rechecked numerically). Hence m(x)=3 ≠ 4=m(y) with d(x)=d(y)=3 refutes (C1), and m(x)=d(x)=3 with the two adjacent coefficients −1, −1 of X³−X−1 refutes (C2)'s forward direction; the sign criterion is refuted under `WeaklySignAlternating`, the most permissive reading of "sign-alternating" (any stricter alternating-signs notion implies it), so the refutation is robust. An iff is refuted by refuting one direction, and the report honestly notes the other direction also fails on paper (X³−X²+X−3, unformalized, not needed).
