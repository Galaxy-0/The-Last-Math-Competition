\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the pseudospectral area of a random matrix is
$\pi\varepsilon^2\,(1 + \tfrac12\|A^*A - AA^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}})$ (an
explicit identity in the Hilbert--Schmidt deviation from normality);
the area growth is exactly $\varepsilon^2$.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

For a normal matrix the singular values of $A - zI$ are
$|z - \lambda_i|$, so $\|(A - zI)^{-1}\|_2 = 1/\min_i |z - \lambda_i|$
and the $\varepsilon$-pseudospectrum is exactly
$\{z : \min_i |z - \lambda_i| < \varepsilon\}$ --- the union of
$\varepsilon$-disks around the eigenvalues. Take the perfectly normal
$A = \operatorname{diag}(0, 10)$: the deviation
$\|A^*A - AA^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}} = 0$ (kernel-certified on the
entries), so the conjecture predicts $\pi\varepsilon^2 (1 + 0) =
\pi\varepsilon^2$. But the true $\varepsilon$-pseudospectrum is two
\emph{disjoint} disks ($2\varepsilon = 2 < 10$), of total area
$2\pi\varepsilon^2$ --- factor 1 claimed, factor 2 true. Grid
integration measures $6.281 \approx 2\pi = 6.283$ against the claimed
$\pi \approx 3.142$. Worse, no formula of the claimed scalar shape
can hold: $\operatorname{diag}(0,10)$ and $\operatorname{diag}(0,5,10)$
share deviation $0$ but have areas $2\pi\varepsilon^2$ and
$3\pi\varepsilon^2$.

The growth clause fails for non-normal matrices: the nilpotent Jordan
block $J_4$ satisfies $\|(J_4 - z)^{-1}\| \sim |z|^{-4}$, so its
$\varepsilon$-pseudospectrum contains a disk of radius
$\sim \varepsilon^{1/4}$ and the area scales like
$\varepsilon^{2/4} = \varepsilon^{1/2}$, not $\varepsilon^2$;
measured: $\mathrm{area}(0.2)/\mathrm{area}(0.05) \approx 2.5$,
matching $(0.2/0.05)^{1/2} = 2$, not $16$.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} certifies the vanishing deviation of
$\operatorname{diag}(0,10)$, performs the grid integration of the
$2$-norm pseudospectrum ($6.281$ vs $2\pi$ analytic, vs claimed
$\pi$), and measures the $J_4$ growth ratio. The Lean package (core
Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies the exact entry arithmetic:
\texttt{diag\_normal} ($A^*A = AA^* = \operatorname{diag}(0,100)$),
\texttt{claimed\_factor} ($1 + 0/2 = 1$),
\texttt{two\_disks\_disjoint} ($2 \cdot 1 < 10$),
\texttt{actual\_factor}, and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted} ($1 \ne 2$
assembly). All five audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend
on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the instance's normality, the vanishing
deviation, the disjointness of the two pseudospectral disks, and the
factor contradiction; the singular-value formula for normal matrices
and the Jordan-block $\varepsilon^{2/n}$ scaling are classical,
cited in prose and reproduced numerically. Both the explicit identity
and the growth clause are refuted.
