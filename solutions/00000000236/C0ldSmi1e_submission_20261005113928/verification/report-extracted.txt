1 The original assertion and the result
Both language versions of conjecture 00000000236 deﬁne the same maximum D(n) and assert the
conjunction of the following statements:
1. For every positive integer n divisible by 4, there is a real Hadamard matrix of order n.
2. The real sequence D(n)/n n/2 tends to 1 as n → ∞ .
A real Hadamard matrix has entries in {−1, 1}and satisﬁes HH T = nIn. The divisibility condition
in the ﬁrst clause does not restrict the sequence in the second clause. The exponent n/ 2 is real
division, including at odd orders. The class deﬁning D(n) is ﬁnite and nonempty, so its maximum
exists and is attained.

5 Formal correspondence and veriﬁcation
The Lean project uses Lean 4.19.0 and Mathlib v4.19.0. Its namespace is Conjecture236. The
source-to-formalization correspondence is as follows:
• SignMatrix n is the ﬁnite type of Boolean matrices. integerMatrix sends its entries to the
integers 1 and −1, and realMatrix casts those entries to R. sign_encoding_complete
proves that every arbitrary real sign matrix occurs in this encoding.
• D is the ﬁnite maximum of absolute determinants over the whole encoded class. The
theorem D_isMaximum proves both attainment by a real sign matrix and domination of
every real sign matrix’s absolute determinant. Thus the ﬁnite representation preserves the
source’s maximum.
• IsHadamard uses real sign entries and the actual matrix equation HH T = nIn.
isHadamard_iff_rows identiﬁes that equation with the usual row scalar products.
HadamardConjecture quantiﬁes over all positive natural orders divisible by four.
• normalizedMaximum uses real exponentiation with exponent (n : R)/ 2. normalization_pos
and normalization_square verify the positive denominator and its exact square for
positive orders.
• AllOrdersLimit is ordinary ﬁlter convergence of this sequence to 1. The theorem
allOrdersLimit_iff_positive_epsilon proves its equivalence to the positive-index ε–N
formulation. A natural-number index at zero is merely a ﬁnite extension and is never used
to refute the limit.
The spectral and parity estimates culminate in sign_determinant_gap; the corresponding
estimate for the true maximum is normalizedMaximum_odd_gap. not_AllOrdersLimit contradicts
convergence by choosing the odd order 2N + 3 beyond an arbitrary eventual threshold N . The
deﬁnition OriginalConjecture is the conjunction of HadamardConjecture and AllOrdersLimit.
The ﬁnal theorem conjecture236_false proves ¬ OriginalConjecture.
