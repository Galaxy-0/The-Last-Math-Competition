Independent mathematical argument

1. The standard finite q-Pochhammer polynomial is

     P(q,N)(z) = product_{k=0}^{N-1} (1 - z q^k).

   For q>0 every q^k is nonzero. The k-th factor is the nonzero constant
   -q^k times z-q^{-k}; its root multiset is therefore the singleton
   {q^{-k}}. The roots of the nonzero finite product are the multiset sum of
   these singleton multisets. Consequently the actual complex root multiset
   contains q^{-k} for k=0,...,N-1, with all multiplicities represented, and
   its cardinality is N. This conclusion does not assume an arbitrary list
   of roots, squarefreeness, or simplicity. Nonzeroness of every factor is
   separately proved by evaluating at z=0, which gives 1.

2. Since q>0, the root q^{-k} is positive real and its complex modulus is
   q^{-k}. The product of all moduli is

     M(q,N) = product_{k<N} q^{-k} = q^{-N(N-1)/2}.

   The summation formula sum_{k<N} k=N(N-1)/2 is proved with real arithmetic
   by induction; there is no natural-number division in this exponent.
   For N>0 the nonnegative N-th root is exactly

     g(q,N) = M(q,N)^{1/N} = q^{-(N-1)/2}.

   Separate Lean theorems establish nonnegativity of M and g and the identity
   g(q,N)^N=M(q,N) for N>0, confirming the intended N-th-root convention.

3. The proposed main term A(q,N)=q^{-(N-1)/2}/(1+q) is positive for q>0.
   Its relative error is therefore exactly

     E(q,N) = g(q,N)/A(q,N)-1 = (1+q)-1 = q,

   for every positive integer N, not only asymptotically.

4. For a fixed 0<q<1, put r=q^{1/2}. Then 0<=r<1 and
   B(q,N)=q^{N/2}=r^N tends to zero along all natural-number indices.
   This uses real exponents and the ordinary theorem on powers of a fixed
   number in [0,1); no even-index substitution or subsequence is taken.

5. If the source's relative Big-O bound held, there would be C>0 and N0>=1
   such that |E(q,N)|<=C B(q,N) for every N>=N0. Since C B(q,N) tends to 0
   and q>0, eventually C B(q,N)<q. Intersecting this eventual condition with
   N>=N0 gives q<=C B(q,N)<q, a contradiction. This works for every admissible
   q. The final negation of the universally quantified necessary clause
   instantiates this proved general failure at the exact rational q=1/2.

All definitions below are in namespace Conjecture7668.

- qPochhammer : Real -> Nat -> Polynomial Complex constructs the product
  from Polynomial.C, Polynomial.X, subtraction, multiplication, and Finset.prod.
  qPochhammer_eval proves its evaluation is exactly product_{k<N}(1-z q^k).
  q is included into C by Complex.ofReal. There is no surrogate polynomial.

- rootModulusProduct : Real -> Nat -> Real is the multiset product of the
  Complex norm over (qPochhammer q N).roots. Mathlib Polynomial.roots is the
  multiset of actual roots including multiplicities; count_roots identifies
  each count with rootMultiplicity. Complex's norm is sqrt(re^2+im^2).
  qPochhammer_roots and qPochhammer_roots_card prove the actual multiset and
  its size, with q != 0, which follows from every admissible source q.

- geometricMean : Real -> Nat -> Real is
  (rootModulusProduct q N)^(1/(N:Real)), using Real.rpow. This is the standard
  nonnegative real root, as additionally checked by geometricMean_nonneg and
  geometricMean_pow. N=0 is merely a total-function extension; every claim
  about the source's N-th root and relative error requires N>0. The eventual
  threshold in RelativeAsymptotic is explicitly at least 1.

- claimedMainTerm : Real -> Nat -> Real is
  q^(-((N:Real)-1)/2)/(1+q). The sign, real subtraction, real half-exponent,
  and factor 1/(1+q) are exactly retained.

- errorScale : Real -> Nat -> Real is q^((N:Real)/2), with real division.
  It is not q^(N/2) with a rounded natural-number exponent.

- relativeError : Real -> Nat -> Real is geometricMean/claimedMainTerm-1.
  Thus the claim concerns error inside multiplicative parentheses, not an
  unnormalized additive error. The main term's nonzeroness used in the
  cancellation is proved from q>0 and 1+q>0.

- RelativeAsymptotic(q) is the proposition
    exists C:Real, 0<C and exists N0:Nat, 1<=N0 and
    forall N:Nat, N0<=N -> |relativeError(q,N)|<=C*errorScale(q,N).
  Each q gets its own C,N0. No uniformity in q is imposed. There is no coupled
  q=q_N limit, finite-prefix test, or restricted subsequence.

- relativeAsymptotic_iff_isBigO proves, for q>=0, that the preceding explicit
  quantifiers are equivalent to the actual Mathlib proposition
    Asymptotics.IsBigO Filter.atTop (relativeError q) (errorScale q).
  It uses the positive-constant version isBigO_iff', nonnegativity of the
  scale, and the characterization of eventual conditions atTop on Nat.
  Therefore removing the harmless N=0 extension is explicitly justified.

- NecessaryClause is forall q:Real, 0<q -> q<1 -> RelativeAsymptotic q.
  This is S from the source's displayed asymptotic, not a claimed equivalent
  formalization of all later prose. not_relativeAsymptotic proves its inner
  clause fails for every source-admissible q. conjecture7668_disproof proves
  Not NecessaryClause; not_relative_isBigO gives the direct Mathlib version.
