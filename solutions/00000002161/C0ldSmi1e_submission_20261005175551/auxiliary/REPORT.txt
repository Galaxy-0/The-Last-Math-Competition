Definitions and assumptions
==========================
A signed graph here is a finite simple undirected graph with a sign +1 or
−1 on each edge. Its adjacency matrix has that sign in both off-diagonal
entries corresponding to an edge and zero elsewhere, including the diagonal.
The independence number is the maximum cardinality of a vertex subset with
no underlying graph edge. Positive inertia index means the number of strictly
positive real eigenvalues of this real symmetric adjacency matrix, counted
with algebraic multiplicity. Negative inertia and nullity use negative and
zero eigenvalues with the same convention. This is the adjacency convention,
not a Laplacian convention and not a minimum over arbitrary matrices sharing
a support. See Liu and Duan [1] for signed adjacency inertia terminology.

Genus is the standard minimum orientable genus over closed connected
orientable surfaces admitting a graph embedding. A cellular embedding has
every complementary component homeomorphic to an open disk. The code builds
one such surface by gluing oriented polygons, checks its topology locally,
and only then applies V−E+F=2−2g. It also reports the Euler genus 2g, which is
zero here. No additional girth, minimum-degree, or 2-connectedness hypothesis
appears in the supplied conjecture. Graph edge signs do not introduce twists
into this standard orientable rotation-system construction.

Exact characteristic polynomial and inertia
===========================================
The determinant of xI−A is computed directly by the 24-term Leibniz formula.
Only five terms survive: x^4, −x^2, −x^2, −x^2, 1. Thus
    det(xI−A) = x^4−3x^2+1.
The exact Sturm sequence obtained by negative polynomial remainders is
    x^4−3x^2+1,
    4x^3−6x,
    (3/2)x^2−1,
    (10/3)x,
    1.
Its final nonzero constant proves the polynomial is squarefree. Its sign
sequences at negative infinity, 0, positive infinity are respectively
    (+,−,+,−,+), (+,0,−,0,+), (+,+,+,+,+).
Ignoring zeros, these have respectively 4, 2, 0 sign variations. By Sturm's
theorem [2], there are exactly two negative and two positive roots. The
constant term is 1, so zero is not a root. Therefore the inertia is (2,2,0).
Here every root is simple, so the distinct-root count is also the count with
multiplicity. More generally the code removes zero factors and recursively
uses p/gcd(p,p') and gcd(p,p') to count multiplicities in separate squarefree
layers. Its K3 boundary case explicitly verifies the repeated eigenvalue −1
is counted twice. Symmetry also places these eigenvalues in the usual real
symmetric spectral-theorem setting; the Sturm count already exhausts all
four roots of the characteristic polynomial.

Genuine orientable cellular embedding certificate
================================================
Write uv for the directed dart (u,v). The six darts are
    01, 10, 12, 21, 23, 32.
The edge-reversal involution and vertex rotation are
    alpha = (01 10)(12 21)(23 32),
    sigma = (01)(10 12)(21 23)(32).
Every alpha orbit has length two and gives exactly one prescribed edge.
Every sigma orbit is exactly the star of one prescribed vertex, with no
missing or repeated dart. The action generated by alpha and sigma is
transitive. The face successor is phi = sigma composed with alpha, namely
    phi = (01 12 23 32 21 10).
Every consecutive pair of face darts has the requisite matching endpoint.
There is one face, using each dart exactly once. Our vertex rotations have
order at most two, so sigma^−1=sigma; either of the common inverse conventions
for face tracing produces this same face cycle.

Construct one oriented closed hexagon whose successive boundary sides are
01,12,23,32,21,10. Glue side 0 to side 5, side 1 to side 4, and side 2 to
side 3, reversing their boundary orientations and preserving graph labels.
Its corner labels in cyclic order are 0,1,2,3,2,1. The checker computes all
corner identifications using union-find, obtaining exactly these classes:
    {corner 0}, {corners 1,5}, {corners 2,4}, {corner 3}.
Thus there are exactly the intended four graph vertices, with no unintended
identification; edge-interior identifications give precisely three graph
edges, with no boundary sides left unpaired.

For each corner, an incoming/outgoing pair of link flags is joined by a
corner arc. The side identifications join these flags across paired edges.
The checker records all 12 link edges with their endpoints. Every link flag
has degree two, and the connected components form exactly one cycle at each
quotient vertex: the flag counts are 2,4,4,2 for vertices 0,1,2,3. A 2-flag
link has two parallel arcs and is still a circle. There are no other link
components. Thus each quotient vertex has a disk neighborhood; paired edge
interiors have disk neighborhoods, and face interior points do as well.
The quotient is a compact connected surface without boundary. Opposite
boundary orientations in each side gluing give a consistent orientation.
The graph complement is the unchanged interior of the hexagon, an open disk.
This is a cellular embedding, not merely an Euler expression assigned to a
graph. The standard polygon realization of combinatorial maps is described
in Bottinelli–Ciobanu–Kolpakov [3, Section 2.1].

The verified cell counts are (V,E,F)=(4,3,1). Therefore chi=4−3+1=2 and
g=(2−chi)/2=0. The nonnegative minimum genus cannot be smaller than 0, so
the exhibited embedding proves the minimum is 0.

Semantic concerns and limits
============================
The literal problem says that equality in alpha<=i_+ forces minimum genus 1
apart from complete graphs. P4 meets equality and is noncomplete, but has
minimum genus 0. This disproof of the genus assertion does not require the
stated inequality to hold for every signed graph; the P3 check warns that it
does not. No unmentioned restriction to graphs with cycles or to graphs of
positive genus should be silently inserted. Under a different definition
requiring the ambient surface itself to be nonorientable, rather than the
usual orientable graph genus or Euler genus, a reformulated problem would
need separate interpretation. The standard orientable reading is explicitly
settled here. All trusted mathematics bridging the finite computation to
topology and eigenvalues is stated above; this Python run is not a formal
Lean proof, and does not validate any author's formalization.
