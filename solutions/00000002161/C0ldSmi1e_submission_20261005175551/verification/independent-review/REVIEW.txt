2. What the original statement says and what is refuted

Both source languages give alpha <= i_+ and assert minimum genus 1 apart from complete graphs. Neither adds minimum degree, positive genus, a cycle requirement, 2-connectedness, or a restriction to nontrivial sign patterns. Thus the all-positive signed four-vertex path is within the stated domain. Its being connected avoids any convention about genus of disconnected graphs. Its vertices 0 and 2 are distinct and nonadjacent, so the complete-graph exception does not apply.

The printed purported universal inertia inequality is false in general (P3 has alpha=2 and i_+=1). This is a defect in the problem, not permission to change its equality condition. The candidate handles this correctly: it does not assume that false universal statement as an axiom and does not replace it by a conventional inertia bound. It directly verifies alpha=i_+=2 for P4. That equality makes P4 an equality-attaining graph for the literal condition even though the inequality is not universally valid.

The per-graph reading entails that every eligible graph has minimum genus 1. The final Lean theorem negates that statement already on the connected subclass, using a realized genus 0 to contradict the minimum's lower-bound clause. The class-minimum reading entails that each eligible graph's minimum genus is at least 1. ClassMinimumOneLowerBound expresses that necessary clause, and the second final theorem refutes it with the same witness. The latter is not a direct formal definition of a class infimum; the report must, and does, describe it as the necessary lower-bound clause. Refuting that clause suffices. The witness also shows the class minimum is actually 0 under natural-number genus. Restricting the universal clauses to connected graphs cannot invalidate a counterexample to the unqualified full-domain claim.

3. Signed graph, independence and exact inertia

SignedGraph contains a genuine Mathlib SimpleGraph and a symmetric real matrix. Every edge has value +1 or -1, and every nonedge has value zero, including the diagonal because the graph is loopless. The support cannot omit an edge or add a spurious one. The actual adjacency is exactly the displayed tridiagonal P4 matrix. The source does not use a Laplacian or a matrix with an unrelated support.

IsIndependent is pairwise nonadjacency on distinct set members. independenceNumber takes the supremum of cardinalities over the full finite collection of independent subsets; generic lower and upper specifications are proved. The witness {0,2} gives the lower bound, and a kernel decide proof ranges over all 16 subsets for the upper bound. There is no answer encoded in a definition of independence.

4. Critical assessment of the embedding definition

I did not accept the name HasCellularGenus as evidence of geometric meaning. I checked its prerequisites individually. The RotationSystem data encode a finite dart set and a bijection from darts to all ordered adjacent pairs. dart_injective prevents duplicate directed incidences, dart_surjective prevents omitted graph edges, and dart_adjacent prevents spurious edges. reverse is a fixed-point-free involution, and reversing twice returns the original dart. This implies that each unordered graph edge has exactly its two opposite darts. No multiplicity or endpoint identification can change the underlying simple graph.

The rotation is a permutation preserving tails. vertex_cyclic places all darts with a given tail in a single orbit; tail preservation prevents any such orbit crossing to a different vertex. vertex_nonempty prevents missing vertex stars. Connectedness of the graph plus this full incidence data gives connectedness of the associated map. These are the complete standard orientable rotation-system conditions, not merely the weaker assertion that two arbitrary permutations exist.

The geometric realization can also be checked directly. At each vertex take an oriented disk with attachment intervals in the specified cyclic order. Glue an untwisted band between the two intervals paired by reversal. Interior band arcs realize exactly the graph edges and cannot create extra intersections. The resulting thickening is connected and orientable. Its boundary circles follow rotation after reversal; capping those circles produces a closed orientable surface with disk faces. Every oriented cellular embedding gives such local orders in reverse. For this path, successively adjoining a new vertex disk by one band keeps the thickening a disk; capping its boundary is a sphere. Thus there is a concrete genus-zero embedding independently of a numerical Euler calculation.

I independently opened Gross and Tucker's primary paper, “Enumerating Graph Embeddings and Partial-Duals by Genus and Euler Genus,” ECA 1:1 (2021), Section 2, page 2. It explicitly gives the correspondence between rotations and oriented embeddings; Proposition 2.2 uses rho lambda, with lambda applied first, for the face walks. Page 6 uses V-E+F=2-2g. Source: https://ecajournal.haifa.ac.il/Volume2021/ECA2021_S2B1.pdf ; DOI https://doi.org/10.54550/ECA2021V1S1S1 . This independently supports the stated convention.

The standard correspondence is explained mathematically and cited; it is not proved as a separate theorem about Mathlib TopologicalSpace objects. I consider the finite combinatorial definition faithful and acceptable under the supplied repository requirement to match the conjecture's definitions. The requirement does not mandate one foundational geometric representation of surfaces. This is not an omitted substantive graph hypothesis or an assumption that P4 has the desired genus: the generic encoding includes the local manifold/embedding data from which the surface is constructed, and the particular surface is also identified directly as the sphere. Acceptance must retain this precise formalization boundary; it would be misleading to claim a separate kernel-checked general geometric realization theorem.

For the finite counts, Face is the quotient by the actual SameCycle setoid of rotate*reverse. I inspected SameCycle: it means reachability by an integer power, so it includes every permutation orbit, even fixed points. It is not a support-only cycle count.
