The mathematical Lean project is exclusively ../lean/. Inspection modules and historical review runners are preserved as .lean.txt and .py.txt outside it. They inspect existing declarations and do not prove or supply an assumption to any mathematical theorem. The portable independent-verify.py script reproduces the root expanded audit with the explicit paths documented in ../README.md. Absolute paths in captured logs identify the original local execution environment. Render hashes document inspected pages; the final report.pdf is supplied at the submission root.
