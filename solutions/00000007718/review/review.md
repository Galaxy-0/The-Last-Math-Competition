## Semantic audit
The conjecture is a conjunction whose final clause asserts that the positive ratio |λ₂|/λ₁ of a primitive substitution is, at r = 3, bounded below by the least possible value (3−√5)/2. A least positive value c over a class yields the universal lower bound ρ ≥ c for all positive ratios in that class; the submission refutes exactly this necessary consequence, which is the correct and sufficient way to refute the clause (Section 1 of the report states this logic explicitly). The stronger results — no positive universal lower bound at all, and no least attained positive ratio — are also proved, via the Archimedean property on the family ρ_m = 1/(m+1) → 0.

The counterexample family is concrete and fully computed rather than asserted. For m ≥ 1, σ_m(a) = a^{m+2}b^m c^{m+1}, σ_m(b) = a^m b^{m+3}c^m, σ_m(c) = a^{m+1}b^m c^{m+2} are actual Lean lists of `Fin 3` letters; `incidenceCounts` counts letters by `List.count`, and `incidenceMatrix_substitution` proves M_m(i,j) equals the letter counts, so the matrix is genuinely the word-count matrix. Every entry is positive, so primitivity holds at the first iterate under the standard definition (`PrimitiveSubstitution`: every letter in every k-th iterate of every single letter; also certified by all-positive M^1). The characteristic polynomial is *computed* in Lean (`incidence_charpoly` via `Matrix.det_fin_three`), spectrum membership is derived from the resolvent determinant, and ordering is proved by `IsGreatest` on the actual complex spectrum moduli: λ₁ = q = 3m+3 (with strictly positive eigenvector (1,1,1)), |λ₂| = 3, so ρ_m = 3/(3m+3) = 1/(m+1). At m = 2: spectrum {9,3,1}, ρ = 1/3, and 1/3 < (3−√5)/2 is proved exactly from 5 = (√5)² and 45 < 49 — no floating point. All of this matches my independent numpy verification to machine precision.

The quantifier structure is handled in the right direction for a disproof: `AdmissibleRatio` existentially quantifies a substitution together with *extra* certificates — nonempty images, an honest geometric realization by colored unit intervals (coverage and disjoint interiors proved over ℝ, `Tiling.lean`), incidence rank three, and a simple strictly ordered positive spectrum with the ratio equal to the second-greatest modulus over the Perron value. These requirements cut out a subclass of the conjecture's primitive substitutions; any universal minimum over the full class restricts to this subclass, so a counterexample in the subclass (satisfying more hypotheses than the conjecture imposes) refutes the universal claim. The negated statement `ConjecturedPositiveLowerBound` is stated in exactly the form ∀ρ, AdmissibleRatio ρ → 0 < ρ → (3−√5)/2 ≤ ρ, and `conjecture_00000007718_false` refutes it. The reading r = 3 is triangulated (three letters, 3×3 matrix, and rank three), so the undefined r in the source cannot hide the counterexample. The other conjuncts of the conjecture (O(|λ₂|^k) error decay, algebraic degree ≤ r−1) are not settled, and do not need to be: a single false conjunct falsifies the conjunction.
