## Semantic audit
The conjecture's first clause asserts a support property of entropy-maximizing chains under transition constraints. The submission instantiates a perfectly legitimate constraint of the conjecture's kind: order k = 1, three states, admissible matrix A with zero diagonal (no self-transitions). Since the conjecture is stated as a general law over constraints, refuting it at one valid constraint refutes it; and since the full statement is a conjunction, refuting the support clause suffices.

The mathematical content is a complete two-sided argument. Upper bound: every admissible row puts all its mass on the two off-diagonal coordinates, so each row law's entropy is a binary entropy ≤ log 2 (Lean: `entropy_le_log_two_of_zero` via Mathlib's `binEntropy_le_log_two`); the proved Shannon chain rule (`entropy_joint`, derived from actual masses with the 0·log 0 = 0 convention) then gives H(q_n) ≤ H(ρ) + n·log 2 for every initial law ρ and every admissible kernel, with no stationarity of competitors and no assumption that their rate limits exist; hence every entropy-rate limit that does exist is ≤ log 2. Witness: uniform π with P(i,j) = 1/2 for i ≠ j is stationary (πP = π, proved), each row entropy is exactly log 2, so H(q_n) = log 3 + n·log 2 and the rate limit exists and equals log 2 — the global maximum. Support: P has two positive entries in row 0 (`transition_not_partialPermutation`), so the support is not a partial permutation matrix. Stronger: any row-stochastic kernel with at most one positive entry per row has all rows pure point masses (`eq_pure_of_unique_successor`), so H(q_n) = H(ρ) for all n and the rate is 0 for every initial law; since log 2 > 0 is attained, no partial-permutation-support kernel can be an entropy maximizer (`no_partialPermutation_maximizer`).

Faithfulness of the formalization: `HasEntropyRate` is the genuine limit of normalized block entropies H(q_n)/(n+1) — the standard entropy-rate definition (the one in the cited Han et al. survey, equation (1)) — not the stationary weighted-row formula (which is instead derived as `block_entropy`/`stationary_hasEntropyRate` under a stationarity hypothesis). The block laws q_n are the actual recursively generated trajectory PMFs, prefix-consistent and Markov-conditioned on positive histories (`blockLaw_consistent`, `blockLaw_markov`), and identified with ordinary time-indexed tuples through an explicit equivalence preserving entropy (`wordEquiv`, `tupleLaw_entropy`). `Admissible K := ∀ i, K i i = 0` encodes support ⊆ A, and `PartialPermutationSupport` is exactly "at most one positive entry per row and per column", the standard partial permutation support notion (about positive transitions, not their sizes — matching the report's discussion). `MaximizesEntropyRate π K h` requires admissibility, an actual rate limit h, and h ≥ the rate of every admissible competitor with a rate — the natural reading of "maximal entropy under given transition constraints", with the comparison domain generously enlarged to non-stationary chains, which only strengthens the witness's maximality. The refuted proposition `SupportConjecture` is the universal reading ("every maximizer has partial-permutation support"), and the separate theorem `no_partialPermutation_maximizer` also kills the weaker existential reading ("some maximizer does"); the witness is additionally proved genuinely first-order (`stationary_pair_not_independent`: joint(0,0) mass 0 vs 1/9 under independence), closing the "really order zero" loophole. The second conjunct (permutation-block formula for the extremum) is honestly reported as not separately formalized, which is unnecessary for the disjunction.
