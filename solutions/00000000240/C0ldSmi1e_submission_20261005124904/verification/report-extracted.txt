Abstract
The product of a segment and a three-dimensional cross-polytope is a four-dimensional
origin-symmetric convex body with Mahler volume 32/3 = 4 4/4!. It has twelve extreme points,
whereas a four-dimensional cube and cross-polytope have sixteen and eight. Thus it attains
the stated bound without belonging to either claimed equality class. The accompanying Lean
project proves the actual body, polar, Lebesgue volumes and obstruction to every linear image.
The conclusion concerns the equality classiﬁcation in the repository’s statement; it makes no
claim to resolve the general Mahler lower-bound problem.

space is invertible. This also rules out every singular map.
The equality and the two non-image conclusions contradict the necessary equality condition asserted
in both language versions. Therefore conjecture 00000000240, with its stated equality classiﬁcation,
is false.
5 Formalization and reproducible checks
The Lean project uses Lean 4.19.0 and Mathlib v4.19.0, with the exact dependency revisions
recorded in its manifest. Its ambient space is E = R × (Fin 3 → R), with ordinary product
Lebesgue measure. The four coordinates are indexed by Option (Fin 3) : the distinguished coor-
dinate is the ﬁrst factor, and the other three are the second factor. The code proves the ambient
dimension, expands the coordinate map and Euclidean dot product, and identiﬁes the transported
cube and cross-polytope with the inequalities used above. The coordinate equivalence is also proved
to preserve volume.
