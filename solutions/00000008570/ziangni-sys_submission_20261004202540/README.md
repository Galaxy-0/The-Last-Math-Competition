# Counterexample to 00000008570

On the actual infinite-dimensional Hilbert space H = l²(N,C), I and 2I are joined by the norm-continuous path (1+t)I of invertible Fredholm operators. Both have index zero, but their actual Fredholm essential spectra are respectively {1} and {2}. Thus the exact essential spectrum is not constant on a connected component and cannot, together with the index, classify those components.

The complete classification clause appears in both language versions. The counterexample preserves the true componentwise constancy of the Fredholm index. It does not claim anything about replacing the exact essential spectrum by an unspecified equivalence class, or about the separate family-index assertions.

Lean constructs the genuine lp Hilbert space and proves it infinite-dimensional from its standard Hilbert basis. The standard Fredholm predicate is explicitly defined using closed range and finite-dimensional kernel and cokernel; no bundled Fredholm theory is assumed. All scalar essential spectra are derived from this definition. The joining path lies in the actual Fredholm subtype, with its inherited operator-norm topology, and the conclusion uses its actual connectedComponent.
