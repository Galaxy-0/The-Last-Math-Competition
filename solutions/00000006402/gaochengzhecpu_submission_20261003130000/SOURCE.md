# 00000006402

**English.** Definition: A difference-closed family is a set family closed under symmetric difference. Conjecture: The atom count is a power of two and the lower bound of the generator count is logarithmic, attained by independent atom families. (power-of-two atoms of difference-closed families)

**中文。** 定义：差封闭族为对对称差封闭的集族。猜想：原子数为二的幂型且生成元数的下界为对数；且下界的达到为独立原子族。（差封闭族原子二幂）
