## Semantic audit
The conjecture as written says that any n points on an irreducible real algebraic curve determine at least c·n^(4/3) distinct distances, with c > 0 independent of n. The submission's reading section isolates the two weakest natural quantifier orders: (U) one uniform constant for all curves and point sets, and (C) a per-curve constant with the bound required only for large n; refuting (C) refutes every stronger order. The counterexample is the line L = Z(y): the polynomial y is prime in ℝ[x,y], hence irreducible, and Z(y) is infinite, so L satisfies the definition of an irreducible real algebraic curve exactly as the conjecture states it — the text does not exclude lines or circles. The n points (0,0),…,(n−1,0) lie on L and their pairwise Euclidean distances are the integers 1,…,n−1, so at most n−1 distinct distances arise; since n − 1 < c·n^(4/3) whenever n > c⁻³, every positive constant fails for all large n under both readings.

The Lean formalization uses the conjecture's own objects, not a surrogate: the plane is `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 2)` with the Euclidean metric, a curve is the zero set of an `MvPolynomial (Fin 2) ℝ` with `Irreducible f` and infinite zero set, the x-axis is proved irreducible via `MvPolynomial.X_prime`, and the distinct-distance count is the cardinality of the image of `dist` on `P.offDiag`. The decisive theorems are exact negations: `not_uniform` is ¬∃c>0, ∀C irreducible, ∀P⊆C, c·|P|^(4/3) ≤ |dist P|, and `not_per_curve_eventually` is the negation of the per-curve eventual reading; `xAxis_violates` supplies the explicit witness family for every n ≥ N. No hypothesis of the conjecture is strengthened and no definition is trivialized (the infiniteness requirement only shrinks the curve class and the line survives it).

The mathematics is correct and was independently sanity-checked: the distances |a−b| for a ≠ b in {0,…,n−1} are exactly {1,…,n−1}, and n−1 < c·n^(4/3) for large n is elementary (the Lean proof: c³n > 1 gives c·n^(1/3) > 1, so c·n^(4/3) > n > n−1). The report's positioning is also honest and correct: Pach–de Zeeuw's theorem gives c_d·n^(4/3) precisely "unless C contains a line or a circle", confirming the line case is the necessary exception that the conjecture text omits; that reading is explicitly not refuted. This is exactly the defect the previous PR #221 was rejected for (pure ℕ arithmetic with no formalized objects), here repaired.
