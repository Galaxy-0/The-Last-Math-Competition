## Semantic audit

The conjecture's first clause asserts a universal property: the h-vector of a quadratic monomial ideal has no three adjacent strictly decreasing terms (the second clause, an appeal to shellability as the source of this positivity, is motivation and is not separately formalized — refuting the first clause falsifies the whole statement). A quadratic monomial ideal is an ideal generated by degree-2 monomials; nothing in the bilingual text restricts to squarefree generators, so (x_0^2, x_1^2, x_2^2, x_3^2) ⊂ K[x_0, …, x_3] is squarely within the conjecture's scope, and it is a complete intersection of quadrics — a genuine, non-toy algebraic object.

The Lean development derives everything from the algebra rather than asserting it: `monomialIdeal`/`IsQuadraticMonomialIdeal` build the ideal from exponent vectors; `hilbertFunction_monomialIdeal` proves the standard-monomial theorem (dim_K (S/I_G)_j = #{degree-j exponents dominating no generator}) by exhibiting the standard monomials as a basis via Mathlib's `basisMonomials` and `mem_ideal_span_monomial_image`; for G4 = {2e_0,…,2e_3} the standard monomials are the squarefree ones, giving HF(j) = C(4, j) and HS = (1+t)^4. Since S/I is Artinian, d = 0 gives the h-polynomial (1+t)^4, and `isHPolynomial_unique` proves uniqueness from h(1) ≠ 0, so the h-vector is well defined: (1, 4, 6, 4, 1), with the strictly decreasing run 6 > 4 > 1 at indices 2, 3, 4 (`h4_tripleDescent`). The decisive theorem `conjecture_false` is exactly the negation of the universal claim, quantified over all n, all quadratic monomial ideals on Fin n, over an arbitrary field K. I verified the arithmetic independently: HF(K[x_0..x_3]/(x_i^2)) = (1, 4, 6, 4, 1, 0, …) — the quotient has the squarefree monomials as its K-basis — so the h-vector is (1,4,6,4,1) and 6 > 4 > 1 is a genuine triple descent.

The submission also handles the literal reading of the definition line ("the h-polynomial … of the Hilbert function of a graded ideal" could mean the Hilbert function of the ideal I itself as a graded module): `idealHF_add_HF` (rank–nullity for S_j → (S/I)_j) and `finrank_homogeneous_four` give HF_I(j) = C(j+3,3) − C(4,j), and `idealHilbertSeries_I4_mul` proves HS_I(t)·(1−t)^4 = 4t^2 − 6t^4 + 4t^6 − t^8 with h(1) = 1 ≠ 0 and uniqueness (`hpoly_unique`); its h-vector (0,0,4,0,−6,0,4,0,−1) has the descent 4 > 0 > −6. I verified the polynomial identity 1 − (1−t²)^4 = 4t^2 − 6t^4 + 4t^6 − t^8 and the coefficient pattern. Both readings are thereby refuted in Lean, and the paper's "Remark (not formalized)" honestly flags that the squarefree polarization J = (x_i y_i) with the same h-vector (1,4,6,4,1) is argued on paper only — it is not needed for the refutation of the statement as written.
