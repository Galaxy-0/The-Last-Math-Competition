# Conjecture 00000008586: the increasing-inclusion clause is false

For the actual Hermitian matrix `T = diag(0,1)`, the number `1/2` belongs to the first range but not the second. A one-column frame `(1,1)^T / sqrt(2)` produces scalar compression `1/2`. For every full two-column isometric frame Q, the determinant of `Q* ((1/2)I - T) Q` equals `-1/4`, so its actual matrix rank is two, exceeding the source's allowed rank one.

The original English and Chinese statements both explicitly assert `Lambda_k(T) ⊆ Lambda_(k+1)(T)`. The main proof uses their printed definition by compression rank at most `k-1`. The standard scalar-compression definition is formalized separately, and the same witness also refutes that direction of inclusion under the standard convention. The source is not silently replaced by a different definition.

## What Lean proves

`OrthonormalColumns Q` is the actual matrix equation `Qᴴ * Q = 1`. The theorem `orthonormal_columns_isometry` proves that such a matrix induces a genuine Mathlib `Isometry` of complex Euclidean spaces. `compression T z Q` is the actual matrix product `Qᴴ * (z • 1 - T) * Q`.

`statedRange` uses existential quantification over all such frames and Mathlib's `Matrix.rank` of the compression. `scalarRange` is kept separate and uses the usual equality `Qᴴ * T * Q = z • 1`. A general theorem proves that the latter set is contained in the source-defined set.

The explicit first-order frame and its normalization are proved exactly. For every second-order frame, the proof derives determinant `-1/4` from the determinant of `Qᴴ * Q = 1`, then applies Mathlib's invertibility and rank theorems. It does not test selected rotations or assume the matrix rank. The theorems `stated_inclusion_fails` and `conjectured_inclusion_false` refute the inclusion actually printed in the problem. The theorem `scalar_inclusion_fails` records the same failure under the conventional definition. `full_counterexample` collects the verified Hermitian witness and both membership/exclusion pairs.

The proof uses `n=2`, `k=1`, and `k+1=2`, all legitimate compression dimensions. The unconditional inclusion is the refuted conjunct. No result is claimed concerning a revised restriction based on the separate generic-nonemptiness statement, its threshold, general spectral envelopes, or arbitrary-dimensional compactness.
