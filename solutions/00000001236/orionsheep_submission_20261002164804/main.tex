\textbf{Conjecture 00000001236 --- verdict: FALSE.}

\textbf{Filed statement (verbatim).} ``Definition: The minimal total weight of
recurrent configurations is the minimum chip count among stable and recurrent
configurations. Conjecture: This minimum equals the max-stable weight minus
the explicit row-minimal sum of a matrix $M$, where $M$ is the rounding of the
inverse of the $(V-1)\times(V-1)$ reduced Laplacian; the minimal configuration
is unique up to isomorphism.''

\section*{The counterexample}
Let $G$ be the path $1-2-3$ on three non-sink vertices, with the sink attached
to vertex $3$ (so $V=4$). The reduced Laplacian (sink row/column deleted) and
its exact inverse are
\[
L=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M_0=L^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}3&2&1\\2&2&1\\1&1&1\end{pmatrix},
\]
and direct multiplication confirms $LM_0=I$. Since
$\det L = 1\cdot(2\cdot2-1) - (-1)\cdot(-1\cdot 2-0) + 0 = 1$, the matrix
$M_0$ is \emph{integral}: every rounding convention (round, floor, ceiling)
produces the same $M=M_0$, so the clause ``$M$ is the rounding of the inverse
of the reduced Laplacian'' is unambiguous on this graph.

\textbf{Both readings of ``row-minimal sum'' coincide.}
(a)~Sum of the row minima:
$\min(3,2,1)+\min(2,2,1)+\min(1,1,1)=1+1+1=3$.
(b)~Minimum of the row sums: $\min(3{+}2{+}1,\,2{+}2{+}1,\,1{+}1{+}1)=\min(6,5,3)=3$.
Under either reading, the quantity subtracted from the max-stable weight
is $3$.

\textbf{The recurrent configurations.} The max-stable configuration is
$c_{\max}=(\deg v_i-1)_i=(0,1,1)$, of total weight $0+1+1=2$. By Dhar's
burning test $c_{\max}$ is recurrent: the sink burns first; then vertex $3$
ignites since $c_3=1\ge 2-1$ (it is adjacent to the burning sink), vertex $2$
ignites since $1\ge 2-1$, and vertex $1$ ignites since $0\ge 1-1$.
Equivalently, $c_{\max}$ has no forbidden subconfiguration. Since
$\det L=1$, the sandpile group of $G$ is trivial, so $c_{\max}$ is the
\emph{only} recurrent configuration; alternatively, the three remaining
stable configurations $(0,0,0)$, $(0,0,1)$, $(0,1,0)$ each fail the burning
test (no vertex can ignite first in the first and third, and in the second the
process stalls after vertex $3$). Hence the minimal total weight of recurrent
configurations is $2$.

\textbf{The conjecture fails.} It predicts
\[
\operatorname{wt}(c_{\max})-\operatorname{rowminsum}(M)=2-3=-1
\]
under \emph{both} readings of the ambiguous phrase, whereas the true minimum
is $2$. The predicted value is even negative, while every configuration weight
is nonnegative. The failure is not an artifact of this one graph: on
$P_2+\text{sink}$ the prediction is $1-2=-1$ (truth $1$) and on
$P_4+\text{sink}$ it is $3-4=-1$ (truth $3$); in all these cases
$\det L=1$ and $L^{-1}$ is integral. The uniqueness clause of the conjecture
does hold here --- the recurrent configuration is unique up to isomorphism ---
but the value identity is false, so the conjecture as filed is disproved.

\section*{Lean formalization}
The pinned core-Lean project (\texttt{lean4/}, toolchain
\texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.33.1}, no Mathlib) formalises with zero axioms and
zero \texttt{sorry}: the entrywise identity $LM=I$ (so $M$ is the exact, hence
rounded, inverse); $\det L=1$; both readings of the row-minimal sum equal
$3$; recurrence on this graph via Dhar's burning test (a parallel monotone
closure in which the sink burns from the start); that $(0,1,1)$ is the unique
stable recurrent configuration; and that the minimum recurrent weight is $2$,
so the conjectured value $-1$ fails under either reading.

\textbf{Reproduction.} In \texttt{lean4/} run \texttt{lake build} and
\texttt{lake env lean Check.lean}; the latter prints
\texttt{\#print~axioms} for every theorem, each reported as depending on no
axioms. \texttt{reproduce.py} performs the exact-arithmetic recomputation
(\texttt{fractions.Fraction}) and cross-checks recurrence with two independent
classical criteria (Dhar burning and forbidden subconfigurations) on three
graphs.
