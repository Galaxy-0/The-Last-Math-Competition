\begin{abstract}
Conjecture 00000001465 asserts, as one of its two clauses, that above a
critical parameter there is a convex function whose Moreau envelope is not
differentiable at a minimizer. No such function exists, convex or not: at every
minimizer $x^\ast$ the envelope $M$ satisfies
$M(x^\ast)\le M(x)\le M(x^\ast)+\|x-x^\ast\|^2/\lambda$, so it is differentiable
there with derivative $0$. The refutation is formalized in Lean~4 against
Mathlib.
\end{abstract}

\section{The conjecture as stated}

The original text of conjecture 00000001465 reads:

\begin{quote}
Definition: The Moreau envelope of a convex function $f$ is
$M_\lambda f(x) = \inf_y\{f(y) + |x-y|^2/(2\lambda)\}$. Conjecture: The critical
parameter for $M_\lambda f$ to be $C^1$ is $\lambda^\ast(f) = 1/L(f)$, where
$L(f)$ is the minimal Lipschitz constant of $f$; for $\lambda < \lambda^\ast$,
all envelopes are $C^1$, while for $\lambda > \lambda^\ast$ there exists $f$
whose envelope is nondifferentiable at a minimizer.
\end{quote}

\section{Reading of the statement}

Functions are real-valued on $\mathbb R^n$, the parameter is $\lambda>0$ as
usual, and $M_\lambda f(x)$ is the infimum in the definition (a real number).
The conjecture is the conjunction of two clauses:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item for $0<\lambda<\lambda^\ast(f)=1/L(f)$, the envelope of every convex $f$ is
    $C^1$;
  \item there exist a convex $f$ and $\lambda>\lambda^\ast(f)$ such that
    $M_\lambda f$ is not differentiable at some minimizer of $M_\lambda f$.
\end{enumerate}
We refute clause~2, which refutes the conjecture. Our argument shows more:
\emph{every} envelope, of any function and for any $\lambda>0$, is
differentiable at each of its minimizers. In particular the choice of
$\lambda^\ast$ and the convexity of $f$ play no role. If ``minimizer'' is read
as a minimizer of $f$ rather than of $M_\lambda f$, nothing changes. Since
$M_\lambda f\le f$ and $M_\lambda f\ge\inf f$, a minimizer of $f$ is also a
minimizer of $M_\lambda f$.

\section{Result}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Let $f\colon E\to\mathbb R$ on a real normed space $E$, let $\lambda>0$, and let
$M=M_\lambda f$ be real-valued. If $x^\ast$ minimizes $M$, then for all $x$
\[
  M(x^\ast)\;\le\;M(x)\;\le\;M(x^\ast)+\frac{\|x-x^\ast\|^2}{\lambda}.
\]
Consequently $M$ is (Fr\'echet) differentiable at $x^\ast$ with derivative $0$,
and Conjecture 00000001465 is false.
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof}

The left inequality is the minimality of $x^\ast$. Taking $y=x$ in the
infimum gives $M\le f$, hence $f(y)\ge M(y)\ge M(x^\ast)$ for every $y$. From
$\|x-y\|\le\|x-x^\ast\|+\|x^\ast-y\|$ we get
$\|x-y\|^2\le2\|x-x^\ast\|^2+2\|x^\ast-y\|^2$, so for every $y$
\begin{align*}
  M(x)&\le f(y)+\frac{\|x-y\|^2}{2\lambda}
  \le 2\Bigl(f(y)+\frac{\|x^\ast-y\|^2}{2\lambda}\Bigr)-f(y)
     +\frac{\|x-x^\ast\|^2}{\lambda}\\
  &\le 2\Bigl(f(y)+\frac{\|x^\ast-y\|^2}{2\lambda}\Bigr)-M(x^\ast)
     +\frac{\|x-x^\ast\|^2}{\lambda}.
\end{align*}
Thus $\frac12\bigl(M(x)+M(x^\ast)-\|x-x^\ast\|^2/\lambda\bigr)$ is a lower bound
for the values $f(y)+\|x^\ast-y\|^2/(2\lambda)$, whose infimum is $M(x^\ast)$.
Hence it is at most $M(x^\ast)$, which is the right inequality. Finally
$|M(x^\ast+h)-M(x^\ast)|\le\|h\|^2/\lambda=o(\|h\|)$.

\section{Formalization}

The accompanying Lean~4 project (\texttt{lean/Submission/Basic.lean}) states
the conjecture and proves its negation against Mathlib.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{IsMoreauEnvelope f lam M} --- for every $x$, $M(x)$ is the
    greatest lower bound (\texttt{IsGLB}) of
    $\{f(y)+\|x-y\|^2/(2\lambda)\}$.
  \item \texttt{lipConst f} --- the least Lipschitz constant;
    \texttt{ConjectureHolds} --- clauses~1 and~2 on
    \texttt{EuclideanSpace} $\mathbb R$ \texttt{(Fin n)}, with \texttt{ConvexOn},
    \texttt{ContDiff} $\mathbb R$ \texttt{1} and \texttt{DifferentiableAt}.
  \item \texttt{le\_of\_isMin} --- the right inequality of
    Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, on any real normed space;
    \texttt{hasFDerivAt\_of\_isMin} --- \texttt{HasFDerivAt M 0 xs}.
  \item \texttt{conjecture\_00000001465\_false} ---
    $\lnot\,$\texttt{ConjectureHolds}, by refuting clause~2.
\end{itemize}

The toolchain is \texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.33.1}, Mathlib is pinned to
\texttt{v4.33.1}, and \texttt{lake build} succeeds. The project contains no
\texttt{sorry}, no \texttt{admit} and no \texttt{native\_decide}, and
introduces no axioms:
\begin{center}
\texttt{\#print axioms conjecture\_00000001465\_false}
\end{center}
reports exactly \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}.

\section{Remarks}

Much more is classical. For a proper lower semicontinuous convex $f$ on a
Hilbert space, $M_\lambda f$ is $C^1$ for \emph{every} $\lambda>0$, with
$(1/\lambda)$-Lipschitz gradient $\nabla M_\lambda f=(I-\mathrm{prox}_{\lambda
f})/\lambda$ \cite{moreau,rw}. So there is no critical parameter at all, and
clause~1 holds for trivial reasons. Our formal argument does not use this
theory, only the elementary estimate of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
