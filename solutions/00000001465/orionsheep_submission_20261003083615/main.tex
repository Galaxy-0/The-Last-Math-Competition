\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} the Moreau envelope of a convex function $f$ is
$M_\lambda f(x) = \inf_y \{f(y) + |x-y|^2/(2\lambda)\}$.
\textit{Conjecture:} the critical parameter for $M_\lambda f$ to be
$C^1$ is $\lambda^*(f) = 1/L(f)$, where $L(f)$ is the minimal
Lipschitz constant of $f$; for $\lambda < \lambda^*$, all envelopes
are $C^1$, while for $\lambda > \lambda^*$ there exists $f$ whose
envelope is nondifferentiable at a minimizer.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

For convex $f$ the Moreau envelope is $C^1$ for \emph{every}
$\lambda > 0$ --- the classical Moreau regularity theorem ($M_\lambda f$
is $C^{1,1}$ with $(1/\lambda)$-Lipschitz gradient) --- so there is no
critical parameter.  Concretely, $f(x) = |x|$ has $L(f) = 1$ (the
difference $|f(1) - f(0)| = 1$ forces $L \ge 1$; the triangle
inequality gives $L \le 1$), so the claimed threshold is
$\lambda^* = 1$; take $\lambda = 4 > 1$.  The minimizer of $f$ is
$x = 0$, and
\[
M_4(0) = \inf_y \{|y| + y^2/8\} = 0,
\]
attained uniquely at $y = 0$ (lower bound $8|y| + y^2 \ge 0$ with
equality iff $y = 0$; kernel-certified).  Moreover for $|h| \le 4$ the
envelope is the exact quadratic $M_4(h) = h^2/8$ (the lower bound
$8|y| + (h - y)^2 \ge h^2$ holds: for $y \ge 0$ it reads
$y(8 - 2h + y) \ge 0$ with $8 - 2h \ge 0$; for $y < 0$ all terms are
nonnegative), so the difference quotient at the minimizer is
$h/8 \to 0$: differentiable there.  The ``nondifferentiable at a
minimizer for $\lambda > \lambda^*$'' clause fails at this instance.
