# Disprove conjecture 00000000086: log|B(m,p)| is not asymptotic to (m−1)²/2 · log p (already false at m = 2)

- **Object.** B(m,p) is FreeGroup (Fin m) modulo the normal closure of all p-th powers, proved to be the largest m-generated group satisfying x^p = 1: generated by m elements, exponent p, unique lifting of generator maps, and a surjection onto every such group (`B_isLargest`, `hom_ext`).
- **Bound.** B(m,p) maps onto (ℤ/p)^m, so it is infinite or |B(m,p)| ≥ p^m. At m = 2, log|B| / ((1/2) log p) ≥ 4 for every p ≥ 2, so the asymptotic fails; m = 1 and m = 3 fail too, since m > (m−1)²/2.
- **Limit.** Refuted along every filter l ≤ atTop with l ≠ ⊥, including all integers and the primes.
- **Readings.** The strict reading (B eventually finite, then the asymptotic) and the junk-value reading (`Nat.card = 0` for infinite groups) are both refuted.
- **Generality.** The result holds for any family of groups with the "largest" property (`not_asymptotic_of_isLargest`).
- **Not addressed.** The individual instances m ≥ 4 (the universal claim fails at m = 2).
- **Main theorem.** `Conjecture86.conjecture86_false`.
- **Lean.** Lean 4.33.1, Mathlib v4.33.1, 235 lines. Axioms: only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. No `sorry`, no `native_decide`.
