\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} $\mu(\bar G) \le |V| - \omega(G) - 1$ (an upper bound
for the complement); the bound is tight (attained by the complete graph with
empty complement).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

At the conjecture's own tight case $G = K_n$ ($n \ge 2$): $\bar G$ is the
edgeless graph, $\omega(K_n) = n$, and the bound reads
\[
  \mu(\bar G) \;\le\; n - n - 1 \;=\; -1 .
\]
But the Colin de Verdi\`ere parameter of the edgeless graph on $n \ge 2$
vertices is $\mu = 0$ (classical: no edges means no admissible nonzero
matrix), and $\mu \ge 0$ always. The claimed inequality is $0 \le -1$:
\textbf{false} at the very case the conjecture names as attaining equality.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} checks the arithmetic for $n = 2..10$. The Lean package
(core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies $-1 < 0$, $\mu = 0 \ge 0$, and
$\neg(0 \le -1)$ --- all \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}. The
classical value $\mu(\text{edgeless}) = 0$ is cited from Colin de
Verdi\`ere (1990) and standard references.

\section{Boundary}

Only the stated inequality at $G = K_n$ is refuted; no claim is made about
other graphs.
