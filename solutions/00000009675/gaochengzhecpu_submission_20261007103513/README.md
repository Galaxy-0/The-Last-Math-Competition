# Conjecture 00000009675: the totient-degree clause fails at N=105

Any two complex numbers generate a field of transcendence degree at most two over Q. The source's totient clause gives phi(105)/2 = 24 for Gamma(1/105), Gamma(2/105), although 105 has exactly three distinct prime factors. Both fractions are reduced, so both denominators are 105.

## What the formal proof establishes

`two_generated_trdeg_le_two` bounds the actual `Algebra.trdeg` of the actual intermediate field generated by an arbitrary two-element complex family. It uses the genuine surjective evaluation map onto the generated algebra, then the algebraic fraction-field extension and the transcendence-degree tower formula.

`gammaPair` invokes the actual `Complex.Gamma` function at 1/N and 2/N. `gammaField` is `IntermediateField.adjoin` of those values over Q. Exact theorems compute `Nat.primeFactors 105 = {3,5,7}` and `Nat.totient 105 = 48`, then refute the quantified totient clause with N=105. No Gamma value is approximated or assumed algebraic or transcendental.

The refuted clause is about the two numbers explicitly named by the source. The package makes no claim about the larger collection of all Gamma values at denominator N or the separate algebraic independence claims. Passing to a field, instead of merely a generated ring, is part of the Lean proof.
