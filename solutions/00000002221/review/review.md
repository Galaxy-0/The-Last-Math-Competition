## Semantic audit

The conjecture bounds the cardinality of the Stone space of the "Booleanization (complemented-closure algebra) of the spectrum" by `2^(2^d)` with `d = dim R`. The phrase "Booleanization (complemented-closure algebra)" is not standard, and the submission handles this responsibly: rather than picking a self-serving reading, it refutes **four** reasonable readings with the same counterexample — (R1) the Boolean algebra of constructible subsets of `Spec R` (Mathlib's `IsConstructible`, the Boolean subalgebra generated by retrocompact opens, which is the reading suggested by the Definition line about the constructible topology); (R2) the clopen algebra; (R3) `Spec R` itself equipped with the constructible topology (Mathlib's `WithConstructibleTopology`); (R4) any Boolean algebra with a bounded-lattice map into the subsets of `Spec R` whose image contains every singleton (which includes the full power set). The report also states what is not covered (algebras not made of subsets of `Spec R`, e.g. a regular-open reading, and local-ring restrictions).

The formalization is faithful to the objects. The Stone space of a Boolean algebra `B` is modeled as `BoundedLatticeHom B Bool`; a bounded-lattice hom into `Bool` automatically preserves complement because complements in the two-element algebra are unique (`f(¬b)` satisfies the defining equations of `¬f(b)`), so this is exactly the classical `Hom(B, 2)`/ultrafilter description of the Stone space — no weakening. The counterexample ring is `R = K^N`, concretely `(ZMod 2)^3`: `ringKrullDim (Fin N → K) = 0` is proved in Lean (finite product of fields, Artinian, `NeZero N`), the homeomorphism `Spec(∏K) ≅ ⨿ Spec K` (Mathlib's `sigmaToPiHomeo`) gives a finite **discrete** `Spec R` with at least `N` points (the kernels of the coordinate evaluations, with injectivity proved), every subset of it is proved clopen and constructible, and the evaluation map `x ↦ (b ↦ [x ∈ φ(b)])` is proved injective whenever the image of `φ` separates singletons, giving `#Stone ≥ N`. The decisive theorems have exactly the right shape: `¬ ∀ (R) [CommRing R] (d), ringKrullDim R = d → #Stone ≤ bound d`, refuted by the witness satisfying the hypothesis `ringKrullDim R = 0`; the quantifier structure matches the universal claim of the conjecture, and `bound d = 2^(2^d)` as a cardinal with `2^(2^0) = 2`.

The mathematics is correct and I verified the essentials independently: `K^N` is Artinian hence zero-dimensional; its primes are the `N` coordinate kernels, pairwise distinct; a finite discrete space has all subsets open, compact, clopen and constructible, so the constructible and clopen algebras are the full power set and the Stone space of the power set of an `N`-element set has exactly `N` (principal) points — in particular `3 > 2 = 2^(2^0)` for `R = (ZMod 2)^3`. The same failure is noted even under the misreading that counts the Boolean algebra itself (`2^3 = 8 > 2`). This submission is precisely the repaired version of the closed orionsheep PR #219: everything that reviewer called prose-only (the ring, Spec, Krull dimension, the Boolean algebras, the Stone space) is now actual Lean objects, and the corrected Stone-space count (3, not 8) is used.
