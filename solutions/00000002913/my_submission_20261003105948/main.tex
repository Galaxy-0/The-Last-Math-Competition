\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} a degree-$d$ polynomial map on $\mathbb{F}_q$
is injective when $q > d^2$ (finite-field injectivity threshold
spectrum).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Take $d = 2$, $q = 5$: the claimed threshold holds ($5 > 4$), yet
the degree-2 map $x \mapsto x^2$ on $\mathbb{F}_5$ is not injective:
\[
4^2 = 16 = 1 = 1^2 \pmod 5, \qquad 4 \ne 1
\]
(kernel-certified collision).  In fact every square in $\mathbb{F}_5$
lands in $\{0, 1, 4\}$: $0^2 = 0$, $1^2 = 1$, $2^2 = 4$, $3^2 = 9 =
4$, $4^2 = 16 = 1$ --- the image has $3 < 5$ elements.  A complete
enumeration shows more: \emph{no} quadratic map ($a \ne 0$) on
$\mathbb{F}_5$ (all $100$) or $\mathbb{F}_7$ (all $294$) is
injective --- a non-degenerate quadratic over $\mathbb{F}_q$ ($q$
odd) has image of size at most $(q+1)/2 < q$ --- while the threshold
$q > d^2 = 4$ holds for both fields.  The plane version fails
identically: $(x,y) \mapsto (x^2, y^2)$ on $\mathbb{F}_5^2$ has
image $9 < 25$ with $(1,0)$ and $(4,0)$ colliding.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} exhibits the certified collision and
3-element image, enumerates all quadratic maps on $\mathbb{F}_5$ and
$\mathbb{F}_7$ (all non-injective, max image size $(q+1)/2$), and
checks the plane instance.  The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1)
certifies \texttt{threshold\_holds} ($5 > 4$), \texttt{collision}
($4 \ne 1$ and $4^2 \equiv 1^2 \bmod 5$), \texttt{image\_three}
(all five inputs into the pairwise-distinct set $\{0,1,4\}$,
$3 < 5$), and \texttt{conjecture\_refuted}.  All four audited
theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the exact residue arithmetic at the first
instance; the enumeration over all quadratic maps and the plane
instance are exhaustive script computations.  The threshold claim is
refuted at $d = 2$, $q = 5$; the conjecture's ``Chebyshev/Lagrange
inversion'' clause is not needed for the refutation.
