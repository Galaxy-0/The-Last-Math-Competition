## Statement correspondence

It does not invent a meaning for “spectral hull” or “algebraic hull,” or claim an independently formalized conclusion about a hull dimension, the second eigenvector, or the coefficient `0.54…`.

For a real 3-by-3 matrix, three real eigenvalues counted with algebraic multiplicity mean that the degree-three characteristic polynomial has three real roots counted with that multiplicity. The formal predicate uses precisely this root multiset and proves its equivalence to real splitting. If the source instead intended three distinct real eigenvalues, that stronger assertion is also excluded. The interpretation does not permit counting arbitrary repetitions of one eigenvalue beyond its algebraic multiplicity.

Constructing the infinite fixed word is not needed for this necessary assertion: the substitution matrix is determined by the three finite substitution images stated in the source.
