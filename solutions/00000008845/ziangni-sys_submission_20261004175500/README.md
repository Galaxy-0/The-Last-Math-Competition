# Counterexample to 00000008845

The actual continuous linear identity on the real Hilbert line is maximal monotone and generated by the proper continuous convex self-dual Lagrangian L(x,p)=(x²+p²)/2. Its full Fenchel conjugate is computed, with attainment, for every continuous linear functional on the product space. The equality graph L(x,p)=xp is exactly the identity graph. The actual Hilbert adjoint equals the identity, and the inverse graph also equals the graph.

Yet the graph cannot have an antisymmetric extension: its point (1,1) forces 1=-1 under skew symmetry. Conversely any antisymmetric subrelation of the identity has domain contained in {0}, so cannot be densely defined. Both extension directions are covered. Antisymmetry means the skew symmetry of bilinear forms, as indicated by the source; it is not order-theoretic antisymmetry.

The report distinguishes the self-dual convex-Lagrangian construction from the additional adjoint/inverse identities and does not assert a false equivalence between these notions. Only the asserted antisymmetric classification is refuted.
