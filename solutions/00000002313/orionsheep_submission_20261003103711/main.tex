\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the second coefficient of the asymptotics of the
restricted Burnside groups: $\log|R(r,p)| = (r-1)^2 \log p +
c_{r,p}$, with $c$ of the explicit $-\log 2$ type; the coefficient
comes from a corrected Witt formula for Lie algebras.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

Read at $r = 2$, the claimed form is $\log_p |R(2,p)| = 1 + c$ with
$|c| \le \log 2$ --- the ceiling $|R(2,p)| \le 2p$. The published
orders of the actual restricted Burnside groups dwarf this:
\[
|R(2,5)| = 5^{34} \quad\text{(24 digits)},\qquad
|R(2,7)| = 7^{20416} \quad\text{(17254 digits; nilpotency class 28,
derived length 5)}.
\]
The exponent gaps are $34 - 1 = 33$ at $p = 5$ and
$20416 - 1 = 20415$ at $p = 7$: for the conjecture to survive, the
``constant'' $c_{2,7}$ of the $-\log 2$ type would have to contribute
a factor of $7^{20415}$. The underlying structure is the opposite of
$O(1)$: the Witt numbers of the free Lie algebra on 2 generators,
$\dim_m = \tfrac1m \sum_{d \mid m} \mu(d) 2^{m/d} = 2, 1, 2, 3, 6, 9,
18, 30, 56, \dots$, grow like $2^m/m$ (total $1{,}465{,}020$ through
degree 24), and the nilpotency class of $R(2,p)$ itself grows with
$p$ (28 at $p = 7$). A ``corrected Witt formula'' yielding a constant
second coefficient does not exist.
