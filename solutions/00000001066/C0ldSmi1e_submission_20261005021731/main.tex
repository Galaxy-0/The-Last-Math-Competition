\begin{abstract}
The conjecture asserts a cardinality bound for Sidon subsets of
$\mathbb F_p$ and attainment of $\sqrt p$ for prime $p$.
We disprove the exact-attainment clause for every prime: a finite
set has integer cardinality, whereas the square root of a prime is
not an integer. The accompanying Lean project proves the negation
of the full written conjunction under both universal-prime and
existential-prime readings of the attainment clause.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement and interpretation}
The supplied English conjecture states:
\begin{quote}
Definition: A Sidon set $B\subset\mathbb F_p$ (all pairwise differences
distinct). Conjecture: $|B|\le p^{1/2}+1$, and $p^{1/2}$ is attained
for prime $p$ (finite-field Sidon extremality).
\end{quote}
The Chinese version has the same bound and attainment clause, joined
by ``and.'' The exact, unmodified bilingual source is included as
\texttt{conjecture.md}.

We use the conventional difference formulation of the Sidon property:
the map $(a,b)\mapsto a-b$ is injective on
\[
 D_B=\{(a,b)\in B\times B:a\ne b\}.
\]
Thus differences are ordered and diagonal pairs are excluded.
The symbol $p^{1/2}$ denotes the nonnegative real square root, with
the natural numbers $p$ and $|B|$ embedded in $\mathbb R$.
``Attained'' is read as the equality $|B|=\sqrt p$.
Neither language specifies rounding or an asymptotic qualifier.

Write $U(p)$ for the stated bound for every Sidon subset of
$\mathbb F_p$, and $A(p)$ for the existence of a Sidon subset of
cardinality exactly $\sqrt p$. The standard reading is
\[
 C_{\forall}:
 \quad\forall p\text{ prime},\quad U(p)\land A(p).
\]
For completeness, we also treat the weaker prime quantifier
\[
 C_{\exists}:
 \quad\bigl(\forall p\text{ prime},\ U(p)\bigr)
 \land\bigl(\exists p\text{ prime},\ A(p)\bigr).
\]
The argument below rules out $A(p)$ for every prime, so it refutes
both readings without any claim about the truth of $U(p)$.

\section{The cardinality obstruction}
\begin{theorem}
For every prime $p$ and every finite subset $B\subseteq\mathbb F_p$,
one has $|B|\ne\sqrt p$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $n=|B|\in\mathbb N$ and suppose $n=\sqrt p$ as real numbers.
Since $p\ge0$, squaring gives $n^2=p$; this is also an equality
of natural numbers by injectivity of their embedding in the reals.
If $n=0$ or $n=1$, then $p=0$ or $p=1$, contradicting primality.
If $n\ge2$, then $p=n\cdot n$ has a proper divisor $n$, since
$1<n<n^2=p$, again contradicting primality.
\end{proof}

The theorem applies to all finite subsets, hence to Sidon subsets.
It gives $\neg A(p)$ and consequently $\neg(U(p)\land A(p))$ for
every prime $p$. Specializing to the prime $2$ yields
$\neg C_{\forall}$. Universal failure of $A(p)$ also yields
$\neg C_{\exists}$.

\section{Formalization and verification}
The project uses Lean 4.19.0 and Mathlib v4.19.0, with exact
dependency revisions pinned in \texttt{lean/lake-manifest.json}.
Its objects are actual finite subsets \texttt{Finset (ZMod p)};
for prime $p$, \texttt{ZMod p} is the prime field.
All such subsets are finite, so this representation covers the
subsets in the conjecture.

The namespace \texttt{Conjecture1066} contains five definitions:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l}
\texttt{IsSidon}, \texttt{UpperBound}, \texttt{ExactAttainment},\\
\texttt{UniversalConjecture}, \texttt{ExistentialPrimeConjecture}.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{flushleft}
The central theorem is \texttt{nat\_cast\_ne\_sqrt\_prime}.
It uses \texttt{Real.sq\_sqrt} and exact transfer of the
natural-number equality from the reals. The prime-power obstruction
is \texttt{Nat.Prime.not\_prime\_pow}, used at exponent two.
\end{flushleft}
The final theorems are
\begin{center}
\texttt{universal\_conjecture\_false}\qquad and\\
\texttt{existential\_prime\_conjecture\_false}.
\end{center}

A fresh copied project was built successfully. Both the proof
source and \texttt{Check.lean} were replayed with warnings treated
as errors. The audit prints all five definitions and all seven
theorem types and axiom dependencies, and separately inventories
all twelve constants originating in the compiled proof module.
Every theorem depends only on \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
There are no admitted proofs, custom axioms, or
\texttt{native\_decide} calls. The proof uses no auxiliary search
or numerical computation; its sole decidable numeral check
certifies $2\le2$ with ordinary kernel-checked \texttt{decide}.

Reproduction instructions, complete execution and trust-audit
records, and file hashes accompany the project. These are local
validation records; repository acceptance is a separate maintainer
decision.

\section{Scope of the disproof}
The submission refutes literal exact attainment of $\sqrt p$ over
prime fields, hence the written conjunction. It does not prove
or refute the upper-bound clause alone, determine a largest Sidon
set, or address rounded or asymptotic attainment. Replacing the
prime parameter by a prime power, adding a floor, or changing
exact equality to an asymptotic statement would produce different
claims. None of those changes is made here.
