## Semantic audit

The conjecture asserts, for hyperplane arrangements A with matroid M(A), the identity depth OT(A) = rank M(A) − 1 − β(M(A)). The submission formalizes the conjecture's own objects, not surrogates: an arrangement is given by coefficient vectors with defining forms α_i = Σ_j v_i(j)x_j; OT(A) is the k-subalgebra of k(x_1,…,x_n) generated by the reciprocals 1/α_i — literally Orlik and Terao's original construction — with its irrelevant ideal generated by the degree-one generators; the matroid rank function is the honest r(S) = dim span{v_i : i ∈ S}; and β is Crapo's invariant in its standard Whitney subset-sum form β = (−1)^{r(E)} Σ_{S⊆E} (−1)^{|S|} r(S). Depth is the standard supremum of lengths of regular sequences (Mathlib `IsRegular`) in the irrelevant ideal. Nothing is defined to make the claim trivially true or false.

The negated statement is a universal over essential arrangements of distinct hyperplanes (nonzero vectors, pairwise independent, r(E) = n). Restricting the universal only weakens it, so its negation implies the negation of the unrestricted claim; moreover both witnesses satisfy all of the stated hypotheses, so this is a genuine counterexample, not an exploit of the formalization. Witness 1: the three lines x = 0, y = 0, x + y = 0 in k² (char 0), matroid U_{2,3}, rank 2, β = 1, RHS = 0. OT(A) = k[1/x, 1/y, 1/(x+y)] is a subring of a field, hence a domain, and 1/x is a non-unit (by the specialization x, y ↦ T^{-1}, under which OT(A) lands in k[T] and 1/x ↦ T, and T is not a unit of k[T]); hence [1/x] is an OT(A)-regular sequence in the irrelevant ideal and depth ≥ 1 ≠ 0. Witness 2: the four lines x, y, x+y, x+2y, matroid U_{2,4}, rank 2, β = 2, RHS = −1, which no depth value in ℕ ∪ {∞} can attain under any reading — making the refutation robust even to alternative conventions for depth or the OT algebra.

The mathematics checks out independently: I recomputed both β values from the exact formalized sum (agreeing also with the classical β(U_{r,n}) = C(n−2, r−1)), and re-verified the non-unit/regular-sequence argument symbolically. The Lean proofs are honest (the domain argument for the nonzerodivisor property, the localization-lift `IsLocalization.Away.lift` for the specialization, induction on the adjoin generators for the image-in-k[T] claim), and report and Lean match statement for statement. The extra remarks (Proudfoot–Speyer context; the observation that the depth bound needs c_i ≠ 0) are correctly flagged as not used in the proofs.
