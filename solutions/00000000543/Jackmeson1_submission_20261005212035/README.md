# Disprove conjecture 00000000543: the depth of the Orlik–Terao algebra is not rank − 1 − β

- **Claim refuted.** depth OT(A) = rank M(A) − 1 − β(M(A)) for hyperplane arrangements.
- **Witness.** The lines x, y, x+y in k² (char k = 0): matroid U_{2,3}, rank 2, β = 1, so the formula predicts depth 0.
- **Depth ≥ 1.** OT(A) is the k-subalgebra of k(x,y) generated by 1/x, 1/y, 1/(x+y) (literally `Algebra.adjoin k (range 1/αᵢ)` in `FractionRing (MvPolynomial (Fin n) k)`); it is a domain, and 1/x is not a unit (the map x, y ↦ T⁻¹ sends OT(A) into k[T] with 1/x ↦ T). So [1/x] is a regular sequence (Mathlib `IsRegular`) in the irrelevant ideal (1/αᵢ), and depth ≥ 1 ≠ 0.
- **Second witness.** U_{2,4} = {x, y, x+y, x+2y} gives rank − 1 − β = −1, which no depth can equal; this also covers the Artinian reading of the OT algebra (where depth is always 0).
- **Objects.** Rank = `finrank` of spans; β by its subset-sum formula; the depth definition follows our earlier package for 00000000537. Context only: Proudfoot–Speyer (retrieved abstract).
- **Conventions.** The grading is not formalized (the irrelevant ideal is generated by the 1/αᵢ); matroid connectedness is not assumed.
- **Main theorems.** `C543.conjecture_543_false`, `C543.witness`, `C543.not_formula_U24`.
- **Lean.** Lean 4.33.1, Mathlib v4.33.1, 338 lines. Axioms: only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. No `sorry`, no `native_decide`.
