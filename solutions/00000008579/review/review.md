The mathematics is correct on every Gaussian space. On infinite-dimensional Wiener space (the literal subject of the conjecture) every eigenspace is infinite-dimensional: the Lean `multCyl_infinite` proves that for every m there are m linearly independent cylinder polynomial eigenvectors for each n ≥ 1 (block monomials x_{nj}···x_{nj+n−1} built inside dimension n·m, with the dimension-consistency lemma `L_eq_of_isPoly` making L well defined on cylinder polynomials); combined with the standard transfer lemma (N = −L extends −L_d on cylinder polynomials, Nualart §1.4, stated as Lemma 1 of the report) these are genuine L² eigenvectors, so the multiplicity is infinite and cannot equal the finite p(n).
