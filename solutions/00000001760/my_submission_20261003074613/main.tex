\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Definition:} for a finite group $G$, $m(G)$ is the smallest
nonlinear degree of an irreducible character, and $[G:H]$ the minimal
index of a maximal subgroup. \textit{Conjecture:} the general bound
$m(G) \le [G:H]^2$ can be compressed to $m(G) \le [G:H]^{3/2}$; the
compression exponent $3/2$ is optimal, verified by the $\mathrm{PSL}(2,p)$
family.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

The small group $G = C_2 \times A_5$ (order $120$) violates the
compressed bound. The irreducible character degrees of $G$ are those
of $A_5$ --- $\{1, 3, 3, 4, 5\}$ --- each doubled by tensoring with the
two linear characters of $C_2$; the smallest nonlinear degree is
$m(G) = 3$. The maximal subgroup $A_5 \times \{0\}$ has index
$120/60 = 2$, the minimum possible for a proper subgroup.

The compressed bound $m(G) \le [G:H]^{3/2}$ reads $3 \le 2^{3/2}$,
equivalently (for positive integers) $3^2 \le 2^3$, i.e.\ $9 \le 8$ ---
\textbf{false}. Note the original square bound $m(G) \le [G:H]^2 = 4$
holds at this instance ($3 \le 4$): the failure is specific to the
$3/2$ compression, which is therefore not a valid general bound.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} constructs $A_5$ independently as the even
permutations of $\{1..5\}$ ($60$ elements), checks its conjugacy-class
sizes ($1, 15, 20, 24$ --- the two $5$-cycle classes merging by cycle
type), verifies the degree list $(1, 3, 3, 4, 5)$ by the sum-of-squares
identity $1 + 9 + 9 + 16 + 25 = 60$, forms $C_2 \times A_5$, and
evaluates the bound. The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1) certifies
the squared comparison $9 > 8$ and the instance anchors; all six
audited theorems report \texttt{does not depend on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

The kernel certifies the arithmetic comparison; the $A_5$
