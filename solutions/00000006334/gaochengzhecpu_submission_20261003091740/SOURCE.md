# 00000006334

**English.** Definition: Weighing matrices are the weighted orthogonal type. Conjecture: The conversion constant is explicit in the existence window of the weight, the necessary and sufficient condition being the square decomposition of the weight; the sum of the decomposition is the matrix order, even; and the lower bound of the window is weight plus two, attained by conference types. (square-decomposition conversion of weight existence windows)

**中文。** 定义：称量矩阵：带重量的正交型（weighing matrix）。猜想：换算常数为重量存在窗的显式且窗的充要为重量的平方分解；分解的和为矩阵阶且阶为偶；窗的下界为重量加二且界由 conference 型达到。（重量存在窗平方分解换算）
