\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} the exact constant in the lower bound for
centralizer sizes: $C_G(x) \ge c\,|G|^{1/2}$ with $c = \tfrac18$; $c$
is tight, attained by low-order simple groups (e.g.\ involutions of
$A_5$).
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

\textbf{The bound fails.} In $\mathrm{PSL}(2,q)$ (simple for
$q \ge 4$), a generator of a non-split torus has centralizer equal to
the torus, of order $(q+1)/2$, while $|\mathrm{PSL}(2,q)| =
q(q^2-1)/2 \approx q^3/2$; the claimed ratio behaves like
$\sqrt{2}/\sqrt{q} \to 0$. Concretely at $q = 139$: $|G| = 1342740$,
$|C_G(x)| = (139+1)/2 = 70$ for $x$ the image of
$A = \begin{pmatrix}3&4\\2&3\end{pmatrix}$ (multiplication by
$\zeta = 3 + 2u$, $u^2 = 2$, norm 1; order $140$ in
$\mathrm{SL}(2,139)$ with $A^{70} = -I$, so the image has order
$70$), and
\[
64 \cdot 70^2 = 313600 < 1342740 :
\qquad |C_G(x)| \ge \tfrac18 |G|^{1/2} \text{ is \emph{false}.}
\]

\textbf{$A_5$ attains nothing.} Brute force over the 60 even
permutations: the centralizer orders occurring are exactly
$\{60, 4, 3, 5\}$ (identity; 15 involutions with $|C| = 4$ by
orbit-stabilizer, $60 = 4 \cdot 15$; 20 three-cycles with $|C| = 3$;
24 five-cycles with $|C| = 5$). Attainment of $c = \tfrac18$ would
require $64\,|C|^2 = 60$; the actual values are $230400$, $1024$,
$576$, $1600$ --- none is $60$. At the conjecture's own example
(involutions) the bound holds with \emph{strict slack}:
$4 > \tfrac18\sqrt{60}$ (equivalently $60 < 1024$). Even the minimum
$A_5$ ratio $3/\sqrt{60} \approx 0.387$ is three times
$\tfrac18 = 0.125$. ``Tight, attained by involutions of $A_5$'' is
false on its face.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} performs the full $A_5$ brute force (centralizer
