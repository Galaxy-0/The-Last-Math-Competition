# 00000008432

**English.** Definition: Minimality of the weight distribution of support designs of codes. Conjecture: The hypergraph rank of the combinatorial configuration formed by supports of minimal weight codewords is t+1 for a design; the rank is determined by generalized Hamming weights, and the single-valuedness of minimal weights is complete on MDS codes. (hypergraph rank of support configurations)

**中文。** 定义：码的支撑设计的权分布的最小性。猜想：极小权码字的支撑形成的组合构形的超图秩为设计的 t+1；秩由广义 Hamming 权重决定且最小权的单值性在 MDS 码上完全。（支撑构形超图秩）
