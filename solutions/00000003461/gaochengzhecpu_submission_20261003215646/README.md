# Conjecture 00000003461: Disproof

The planar four-cycle has ordinary chromatic number 2 and DP-chromatic number 3: a twisted two-fold cover is uncolorable, while every cover with at least three colors per vertex admits an independent transversal. It attains the claimed gap of one on four vertices, fewer than any polygonal prism graph.

## Scope

This refutes the source's separate assertion that a prism is the smallest planar graph attaining that gap. It does not dispute the planar DP-coloring bounds elsewhere in the statement. The girth-five hypothesis belongs to a different clause and is not imposed on the asserted planar-class minimality.
