\section{The conjecture}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Conjecture:} The exceptions to $g(k) = 2^k + \lfloor (3/2)^k \rfloor - 2$
are finite, with the largest exception $k = 4$ ($g(4) = 19 > 2^4 + \lfloor
(3/2)^4 \rfloor - 2$); for $k \ge 5$ the formula always holds.
\end{quote}

\section{The refutation}

With the conjecture's own value $g(4) = 19$ (the classical theorem of
Balasubramanian--Deshouillers--Dress, 1986),
\[
  2^4 + \lfloor (3/2)^4 \rfloor - 2 \;=\; 16 + \lfloor 81/16 \rfloor - 2
  \;=\; 16 + 5 - 2 \;=\; 19 \;=\; g(4).
\]
The claimed strict inequality is $19 > 19$: false. The formula holds at
$k = 4$ \emph{with equality}, so $k = 4$ is not an exception at all and the
conjecture's stated exception structure collapses.

\section{Verification}

\texttt{reproduce.py} checks $\lfloor 81/16 \rfloor = 5$, the value $19$,
that $19 > 19$ is false, and re-derives the classical witness: the exact DP
shows $79 = 4 \cdot 16 + 15 \cdot 1$ requires $19$ fourth powers while every
$n < 344$ needs at most $19$. The Lean package (core Lean~4, v4.33.1)
certifies the four closed statements ($\lfloor 81/16\rfloor = 5$,
$16 + 5 - 2 = 19$, $\neg(19 > 19)$, $19 = 19$), all \texttt{does not depend
on any axioms}.

\section{Boundary}

Only the $k = 4$ exception clause is refuted (using the conjecture's own
$g(4) = 19$). The finiteness and $k \ge 5$ clauses are separate assertions,
not needed here.
